# 客户流失预警与挽回

> **核心逻辑**：客户不会突然消失，流失总有前兆。早发现、早诊断原因、早介入，挽回一个老客户比开发新客户便宜得多。
> 本文件规范流失信号识别、原因分类、挽回话术与Win-back邮件序列。
> 适用对象：KaiLionCrafts 全部历史客户（尤其A/B类）。

---

## 一、流失信号识别（Churn Signals）

**中文说明**：出现以下任一信号即标记为"流失预警"，触发主管关注，不要等到客户彻底不回才行动。

| Signal 信号 | 预警阈值 | 预警等级 |
|---|---|---|
| **Response Slowdown 回复变慢** | 以往24h回 → 现在3–5天才回，或已读不回 | 黄色 |
| **Order Decline 订单减少** | 复购间隔比上轮拉长50%，或单量逐次下降 | 黄色 |
| **No Inquiry Silence 询价不回** | 发出报价/样品后连续2次跟进无回应 | 橙色 |
| **Competitor Switch 转竞品迹象** | 问"你家价格比别人高""能不能再降"；或海关数据出现新供应商 | 红色 |
| **No Contact 长期沉默** | A类客户90天、B类60天无任何沟通 | 橙色 |
| **Complaint Unresolved 投诉未闭环** | 客户提质量/交期问题后未得到满意解决 | 红色 |

> **自动预警**：CRM按"Last Follow-up 距今天数"和"订单间隔"自动标色，每周一清单推送给负责人。

---

## 二、流失原因分类（Churn Reasons）

**中文说明**：挽回前先诊断原因，对症下药。每次 Lost/Dormant 客户必须在CRM选一个原因标签。

| Reason 原因 | 典型表现 | 挽回可能性 |
|---|---|---|
| **Price 价格** | "too expensive""found cheaper" | 中（可谈阶梯价/降配置） |
| **Quality 质量** | 产品不达标/差评/次品 | 低–中（需先改进+补偿） |
| **Lead Time 交期** | "took too long""missed our season" | 中（优先排产承诺） |
| **Service 服务** | 沟通不及时/问题不解决 | 中（换对接人+老板介入） |
| **Competitor 竞品** | 已转供应商且体验好 | 低（靠差异化+样品再试） |
| **Customer's Own 客户自身** | 客户倒闭/转型/库存积压/季节性 | 不定（等周期再激活） |

## 三、挽回话术（Win-back Scripts by Reason）

**中文说明**：按流失原因选对应英文邮件，语气真诚、不卑不亢、给台阶。

### A. 价格原因

**Subject:** Can we revisit the price?

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I noticed we haven't worked together lately — I'd guess pricing was part of it, and I want to be upfront.
>
> For orders over [volume], we can offer a tiered price that brings the unit down to about [X%] lower. If you're open to it, send me the quantity you're thinking and I'll send a fresh quote within 24h.
>
> No obligation — just want you to know the door's open.
>
> Best,
> Leo

### B. 质量原因

**Subject:** I'd like another chance to get it right

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I take it seriously that the last order didn't fully meet your expectations. We've [changed the QC step / upgraded the steel / improved packaging] since then.
>
> I'd love to send you a free updated sample so you can judge the improvement yourself. If it's not better, no hard feelings.
>
> Would that be okay?
> Leo

### C. 交期原因

**Subject:** Faster lead time for your next order

> Hi [First Name],
>
> You mentioned last time that the lead time ran longer than you needed. We've since built [buffer stock / a dedicated production line], and your category now ships in [X days] instead of [Y].
>
> If timing was the sticking point, I'd be glad to reserve early production for your next run.
>
> Best,
> Leo

### D. 服务/沟通原因

**Subject:** A note from Leo directly

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I sense the communication on your last order wasn't as smooth as it should be, and for that I apologize. I'm now personally overseeing your account.
>
> Whatever went wrong — slow replies, missed updates, an unresolved issue — tell me directly and I'll fix it. You can reach me on WhatsApp anytime: [number].
>
> Warm regards,
> Leo

### E. 竞品原因（不纠缠，留门）

**Subject:** Just want to stay in touch

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Sounds like you've found a setup that's working, and that's completely fine. I won't keep pitching.
>
> I'll only reach out if it's genuinely useful — new products, price changes, or if you ever want a second quote as a benchmark.
>
> Wishing you a great season.
> Leo

## 四、Win-back 邮件序列（3封：Day 1 / Day 7 / Day 30）

**中文说明**：对已沉默/Lost客户，按固定节奏发3封，每封一个目的；任一封回复即停止序列转入正常沟通。

### Email 1 — Day 1：友好破冰 + 诊断原因

**Subject:** Everything alright on your end?

> Hi [First Name],
>
> It's been a while since we last spoke, and I wanted to check in. Is everything OK on your side?
>
> If anything about our last cooperation didn't meet expectations, I'd genuinely appreciate knowing — it's the only way we improve.
>
> Either way, hope business is going strong.
> Best,
> Leo

### Email 2 — Day 7：给价值（新品/优惠/行业信息）

**Subject:** New [product] & a returning-customer offer

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Not to nudge too hard, but thought you'd like to know:
>
> - We just launched [new product / new spec] that fits [their category]
> - As a returning customer, your next order gets [X% off / free shipping / sample credit]
>
> If now's not the time, no worries — just reply "not now" and I'll stop.
> Leo

### Email 3 — Day 30：Breakup 优雅收尾（往往回复率最高）

**Subject:** Should I close your account file?

> Hi [First Name],
>
> This will be my last email for now. I understand if [cutlery sourcing / our cooperation] isn't a priority at the moment, and I don't want to clutter your inbox.
>
> If anything changes — a price comparison, samples, or a backup supplier — I'm one message away. Your account history is still saved on our side.
>
> Wishing you and [Client Company] the best.
>
> Warm regards,
> Leo
> WhatsApp: [number]

## 五、挽回执行纪律

1. **红色信号48小时内**负责人必须主动联系一次，不能等。
2. 每次 Lost 客户**必填流失原因标签**，季度复盘哪些原因最多，反推产品/服务改进。
3. Win-back序列**每月跑一批**沉默客户，不要手动一个个想起来。
4. 挽回成功后**重新走需求确认**，不要假设客户还是当年的需求；重新评估Hotness。
5. 连续3轮Win-back无回应 → 降级D类，转入年度群发，不再占用一对一精力。

> 配套：沉睡/复购触点见《15_客户留存与复购策略》；分级降级见《13_客户分级管理体系》；邮件节奏参考《邮件模板/02_跟进邮件序列》。
