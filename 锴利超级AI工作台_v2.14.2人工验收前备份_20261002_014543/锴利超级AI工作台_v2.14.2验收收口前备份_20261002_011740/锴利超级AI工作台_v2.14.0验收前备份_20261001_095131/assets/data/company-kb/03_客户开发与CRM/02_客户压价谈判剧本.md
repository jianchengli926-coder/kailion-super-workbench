# 实战剧本02：客户压价谈判剧本（3大场景）

> **核心心法**（来自 `09_行业知识与培训/15_B2B谈判技巧与异议处理.md`）：
> 谈判的核心不是"降不降价"，而是"让客户觉得你的价格值，甚至愿意为价值买单"。
> 80%的压价是试探底线、习惯性砍价、怕买亏，不是真实预算限制。盲目降价反而让客户质疑产品价值。

---

## 场景A：客户说"你的价格比竞品高20%"

> **背景**：美国DTC厨具品牌（Persona 3品牌商），询盘 KL-KN-DS-008（大马士革纹理主厨刀黑柄，$12基准价，MOQ 100pcs）。我方报价500pcs $9.60/pc FOB。客户回复说另一中国供应商报$8.00。

### A1. 客户原话

> **Subject:** Re: Quotation KLC-Q-2026-052
>
> Hi Leo,
>
> Thanks for the quote. To be frank, your price is about 20% higher than another supplier we're talking to. They quoted us $8.00 FOB for the same-looking 8" Damascus-pattern chef knife, 500 pcs.
>
> Is there room to move? We'd prefer to work with you based on your LFGB docs, but the price gap is significant.
>
> — David, Chef's Gear USA

### A2. 我方应对（先认同 + 摆差异，不降价）

> Hi David,
>
> I appreciate you being direct — and I totally understand that price is a key factor.
>
> Before I adjust anything, let me make sure we're comparing the same knife. "Damascus pattern" can mean very different things:
>
> | Spec | Our KL-KN-DS-008 | What $8.00 typically buys |
> |------|-------------------|----------------------------|
> | Steel | High carbon stainless, acid-etched Damascus feather pattern | Often 3Cr13 with printed/etched pattern (not real layered steel) |
> | Hardness | HRC 56-58, SGS tested | HRC 50-52, no third-party test |
> | Edge | Hand-sharpened to 15° double-bevel, CATRA-tested | Machine-sharpened, no CATRA data |
> | Tang | Full tang (steel runs through handle) | Often half tang |
> | Certification | SGS LFGB + FDA + TÜV blade report | Often no food-contact cert |
>
> The $8.00 knife may look identical in a photo, but in 6 months of customer use, the edge retention and corrosion resistance tell the real story. As a DTC brand, your reviews are everything — a 1-star "dulls fast" review costs you far more than $1.60/pc.
>
> Would you like me to send a sample of ours so your product team can compare it side-by-side with the other supplier's sample?

### A3. 客户反驳

> Hi Leo,
>
> Fair points. The other supplier did say their steel is 5Cr15MoV at HRC 55. And they offered samples free.
>
> I'm not trying to play you against them — but $8.00 vs $9.60 across 500 pcs is $800 difference. That's real money for a small brand.
>
> Can you meet $8.50? If yes, we go with you.

### A4. 我方最终方案（不裸降，绑条件）

> Hi David,
>
> $8.50 I cannot do on 500 pcs — that's below our cost on this spec. But here's what I can do:
>
> **Option 1 — Volume upgrade to 1,000 pcs:** Unit price **$8.60**, logo included. This brings your total to $8,600 vs $4,000 at 500 — but the unit price is almost exactly where you want it, and you lock in a full production run.
>
> **Option 2 — Keep 500 pcs at $9.60, but I'll:**
> - Absorb the sample cost (2 samples to you, no charge — normally $23)
> - Throw in 10 free spare knives (2%)
> - Include FSC-certified gift box upgrade (normally +$0.40/pc)
>
> The all-in value of Option 2 is roughly equivalent to $8.90/pc.
>
> My recommendation: **Option 2 first** (test the market at 500 pcs). If it sells, your reorder at 1,000 pcs gets $8.60 automatically.
>
> Which direction works for you?
>
> — Leo

**结果：客户选择 Option 2，500pcs @ $9.60，含免费样品+10备品+FSC礼盒升级。样品测试后复购1000pcs @ $8.60。**

## 场景B：客户要求"MOQ从500降到100，价格不变"

> **背景**：英国亚马逊小卖家（Persona 4 DTC电商），想做 KL-KN-SS-014（空心边三德刀不锈钢黑柄，$8基准，MOQ 100pcs）品牌化。我方报价500pcs起、$6.40/pc。客户说首单只能100pcs，要求按500pcs价格。

### B1. 客户原话

> Hi Leo,
>
> Love the santoku knife. But your MOQ is 500 pcs — that's way too much for us to test a new listing. We're a small Amazon brand and want to start with 100 pcs.
>
> Can you do 100 pcs at the same $6.40 price? If yes, we'll order today. If the listing takes off, we'll scale up fast.
>
> — James, KitchenPro UK

### B2. 我方应对（不能既降MOQ又保价，但给台阶）

> Hi James,
>
> Congrats on launching your brand — I remember what it's like to start small.
>
> Here's the honest tradeoff: producing 100 pcs vs 500 pcs means the factory has to set up the line, calibrate the laser logo, and run QC on a short batch — the setup cost gets spread across fewer units. So 100 pcs at $6.40 isn't possible.
>
> But I don't want MOQ to be why you can't start. Here's a workable path:
>
> **100 pcs trial order:**
> - Unit price: **$8.50/pc FOB Shenzhen** (this is our minimum-batch price)
> - Laser logo included
> - **$2.10/pc credit back** on your next order of 300+ pcs — effectively your second order averages out to the $6.40 you wanted
> - Lead time: 15 days (logo customization)
>
> Or, if you want to start even cheaper:
>
> **50 pcs from our US warehouse stock** (no logo, neutral packaging):
> - $9.90/pc delivered to your Amazon FBA UK via our US→EU fulfillment lane
> - Ships in 3 days
> - Test the listing risk-free, then come back for the branded run
>
> Which fits your launch plan better?

> **📚 使用资料标注：**
> - `03_客户开发与CRM/06_谈判话术与异议处理.md` → 二、MOQ异议：话术1灵活MOQ（100pcs试单无logo）；话术3现货
> - `09_行业知识与培训/15_B2B谈判技巧与异议处理.md` → 心法2"变被动为主动——抛新方案"（拆单/试单/现货）
> - `11_订单与财务/04_报价体系与价格策略.md` → MOQ定价梯度（轻定制50-150件；样品价=大货价×1.2-2；现货无MOQ）
> - `03_客户开发与CRM/10_客户画像与买家Personas.md` → Persona 4 DTC卖家：低MOQ是硬门槛；美国仓现货是大杀器；升级路径50→500→1000+

### B3. 客户反驳

> $8.50 for 100 pcs is still high for a test. Can you split the difference — $7.50 for 100 pcs? I really don't want to start with unbranded stock because I want my logo on day one.

### B4. 我方最终方案（守住价格，用服务增值）

> Hi James,
>
> I hear you on wanting your logo on day one — that's the right call for a brand.
>
> $7.50 I can't do on 100 pcs. But here's a middle path that gets you what you need:
>
> **100 pcs with logo at $8.50/pc**, and I'll add at no cost:
> 1. **Free professional photoshoot**: 20+ 4K white-background + lifestyle photos of your logo'd knives (normally $300 value) — ready for your Amazon listing
> 2. **FBA-ready prep**: we label, box, and ship directly to your Amazon UK warehouse — no work on your end
> 3. **The $2.10/pc credit on your next 300+ order** stays on the table
>
> So you're getting: 100 logo'd knives + full Amazon photo package + FBA delivery setup, all for $850. The photo package alone saves you weeks of listing work.
>
> Shall I send the PI?
>
> — Leo

**结果：客户接受100pcs @ $8.50，含免费拍摄+FBA代发+返单credit。Listing 6周后跑通，复购500pcs @ $6.40。**

## 场景C：客户说"我找到更便宜的供应商了"

> **背景**：荷兰厨房用品批发商（Persona 2 Wholesaler），已合作过1单300pcs KL-KN-SS-011（木纹柄不锈钢主厨刀，$6基准）。复购时客户说找到了另一家中国供应商便宜15%。

### C1. 客户原话

> Hi Leo,
>
> We're looking to reorder the wood-handle chef knife (KL-KN-SS-011). To be honest, another factory offered us the same knife at $4.80/pc for 500 pcs, FOB Ningbo. That's about 15% cheaper than your $5.60.
>
> We liked working with you, but the margin difference is significant for a wholesaler. Is there anything you can do?
>
> — Pieter, KeukenBlok BV (Rotterdam)

### C2. 我方应对（老客户+批发商：先问清楚再给方案）

> Hi Pieter,
>
> Thanks for being straight with me — I respect that.
>
> Before I respond, two quick questions so I understand the comparison:
>
> 1. Is their knife the same **full-tang** construction? Our KL-KN-SS-011 is full-tang, high-carbon stainless, HRC 56-58.
> 2. Does their price include **your laser logo** and a **color box**, or is it bare knife / bulk packing?
>
> The reason I ask: for wholesalers, the unit price matters, but so does the cost of a returned container or a batch of complaints from your retail shops. We've supplied 500 sets to German customers with **zero breakage in transit** — that doesn't happen by accident; it's our export carton packing and pre-shipment QC.
>
> That said, you're a returning customer and I want to keep the business. Let me work something out.

> - `03_客户开发与CRM/06_谈判话术与异议处理.md` → 五、竞品对比：话术1不贬低对手，问规格差异；话术3长期合作
> - `09_行业知识与培训/15_B2B谈判技巧与异议处理.md` → 心法1"先认同→摆差异→算长期账"
> - `04_国家地区开发指南/02_欧洲_德英法意西荷.md` → 荷兰客户开放直接、注重效率；鹿特丹是欧洲分拨港
> - `09_行业知识与培训/15_B2B谈判技巧与异议处理.md` → 欧美客户：用案例/认证/返修率数据说话（德国客户500套破损率0）

### C3. 客户回复

> Their price is bare knife, no logo, no color box — just bulk polybag. We'd have to handle logo and packaging ourselves, which adds about $0.60/pc.
>
> Their steel is listed as 5Cr15MoV, but no HRC certificate.

### C4. 我方最终方案（算TCO总账 + 给复购折扣）

> Hi Pieter,
>
> That changes the picture. Let's compare the real landed cost per knife:
>
> | | Their quote | Our quote (reorder) |
> |---|---|---|
> | Bare knife | $4.80 | — |
> | Our all-in price (logo + color box + QC) | — | $5.60 |
> | Your self-managed logo & packaging | +$0.60 | — |
> | Risk: no HRC cert / no QC video | hard to quantify | 0 (pre-shipment video included) |
> | **Real cost per knife** | **~$5.40 + risk** | **$5.60 all-in** |
>
> The actual gap is only $0.20/pc, and you're taking on all the packaging/QC work yourself.
>
> That said, as a thank-you for reordering, here's what I'll do:
>
> **500 pcs KL-KN-SS-011 at $5.30/pc FOB Shenzhen** (down from $5.60) — logo + color box + pre-shipment video included.
>
> If you commit to a **quarterly reorder of 500 pcs**, I'll hold this $5.30 price for 12 months and throw in free DHL sample shipping whenever you want to test new models.
>
> This is my best price for a wholesaler partner — I can't go lower without changing the steel spec.
>
> — Leo

**结果：客户接受$5.30/pc，签季度500pcs框架，12个月锁价。年采购2000pcs，成为稳定B+类客户。**

## 三场景谈判技巧速查

| 场景 | 客户类型 | 核心打法 | 关键话术 | 红线 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|------|
| A. 比竞品贵20% | DTC品牌商（Persona 3） | 价值锚定+参数对比+样品实测 | "Photo looks same, edge retention tells the truth in 6 months" | 不说"别家质量差" |
| B. MOQ降100保价 | Amazon卖家（Persona 4） | 不降裸价，用服务增值（拍摄/FBA/返单credit） | "$8.50 now, $2.10 credit back on next 300" | 不同时降MOQ又保价 |
| C. 找到更便宜供应商 | 批发商老客户（Persona 2） | 算TCO总账+年度框架锁价 | "Real gap is $0.20, not 15% — and you take on all the work" | 不盲目跟价，守住成本线 |

> **通用纪律**（来自 `09_行业知识与培训/15_B2B谈判技巧与异议处理.md` 第六节）：
> - 不问"您心理价位多少"
> - 不说"我跟老板申请一下"
> - 不贬低竞品
> - 不无理由盲目让步
> - 不一次性降到底
> - 跟进最多2次，之后靠私域内容触达
