# 实战剧本07：五国客户开发对话示例

> **用途**：把 `04_国家地区开发指南/` 里五国的"商务特点"落到真实邮件往来上。每个国家一个完整对话示例，8–10 轮邮件/消息，每轮后附策略分析。
> **使用方法**：开发对应国家客户前，先读一遍本国示例，感受对方说话节奏、关注点、谈判风格，再对照 `03_客户开发与CRM/客户常见异议处理话术库.md` 找话术。
> **公司背景锚点**：KaiLionCrafts，阳江刀剪供应链，四大品类（厨房刀具/专业剪刀/户外刀具/厨房用品），127 SKU；付款 T/T 30%+70%、样品 PayPal、大客户可 L/C at sight；美国本地仓可发 FBA 补货。
> ⚠️ 文中客户一律用 "Client A/B/C…" 或 "[Client Name]" 代称；价格/MOQ 均为**示例数据，非公司实际定价**。

## 场景一：美国客户 — 户外刀批发商（Amazon 店铺）

### 客户背景

| 项 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 国家 | 美国（俄勒冈州，西海岸） |
| 公司 | Client A，约 15 人小型批发+Amazon 卖家 |
| 渠道 | Amazon FBA 店铺 + 独立站 + 5 家本地户外用品店分销 |
| 采购需求 | 户外折叠刀/固定刃刀，用于 Amazon listing 铺货，月补货量 500–1000 pcs |
| 关注点 | **价格、交期、美国仓/FBA 贴标、Prop 65/FDA 合规、能不能跟现有供应商比价** |
| 决策人 | Client A 老板本人（采购+运营一肩挑），决策快，1–2 周 |
| 时区 | 美西 PDT，与北京差 15–16 小时；发信窗口北京时间 00:00–09:00 |

> **匹配产品**：KL-OD-SS-004（红木柄不锈钢折叠猎刀，FOB 示例 $1.80/pc @1000pcs）、KL-OD-SS-012（黑白 G10 柄镜面折叠刀，FOB 示例 $1.50/pc @1000pcs）、KL-OD-TI-004（全黑战术固定刃，FOB 示例 $2.20/pc @1000pcs）。
> ⚠️ 美国客户**不卖自动刀/弹簧刀（switchblade/auto knife）**——法律红线，开发信里主动划清，反而显专业。

---

### Round 1 — 冷开发信（北京时间 08:00 定时发，美西 17:00 前一天到达，客户早上看到）

**我方发件：**

> **Subject: Factory-direct folding knives for your Amazon store — US warehouse available**
>
> Hi [Client Name],
>
> I came across your Amazon store — the "Tactical EDC" selection is moving well (nice reviews on the G10 liner-locks). I'm Leo from KaiLionCrafts, a direct cutlery factory supply chain in Yangjiang, China.
>
> For Amazon sellers like you, we offer:
> - **FBA prep service**: polybag + FNSKU label + carton marking, shipped to your Amazon warehouse directly
> - **US local stock**: we keep fast-moving folding knives in our LA warehouse — 7-day restock to FBA, no sea freight wait
> - **Compliance ready**: FDA + Prop 65 warning labels on request; **no switchblades/auto knives** (we know US law)
> - MOQ 100 pcs/model, private-label laser logo from 100 pcs
>
> Would it help if I sent our top-10 Amazon-proven outdoor knife list with FOB prices? No call needed — just reply "send list" and I'll forward.
>
> Best,
> Leo Li | KaiLionCrafts
> WhatsApp: +86 131-3800-6564

**策略分析：**
- **为什么这样写**：美国客户效率至上，24h 必回，讨厌寒暄。开头 2 句点明"我看过你 Amazon 店"（背调证据），不写空话。
- **切入点选择**：Amazon 卖家最痛的不是单价，是**补货断货**。所以主推"美国本地仓 7 天补 FBA"这个差异化卖点，而不是先拼单价。
- **合规主动划线**：主动说 "no switchblades"，证明我们懂美国刀具法规（`04_国家地区开发指南/01_北美_美国加拿大.md` 第六条禁忌第 5 条），降低客户合规顾虑。
- **轻量 CTA**："reply 'send list'"——回复门槛极低，不需要客户写长邮件。
- **发信时间**：北京时间 08:00 = 美西前一天 17:00 定时投递，客户次日早上 inbox 第一条看到。

---

### Round 2 — 客户回复（1 天后，直接要价）

**客户回件：**

> Hi Leo,
>
> Send the list. Also — what's your best price on a 3.5-inch liner-lock folding knife, G10 handle, black blade, 1000 pcs? We're currently paying $2.10 FOB from a Ningbo supplier. Need to know if you can beat that.
>
> And yes we need FBA prep + Prop 65.
>
> — [Client Name]

**策略分析：**
- **客户信号解读**：美国卖家极直接——直接给目标价 $2.10、直接说数量 1000pcs、直接要 FBA prep。这是**真实买手**，不是比价骗子。
- **注意**：客户已经有供应商（宁波），我们是 second source。不要一上来就报"我比你便宜 30%"——先确认规格是否对等。

---

### Round 3 — 我方回复（24h 内，给清单 + 锚定差异）

**我方回件：**

> Hi [Client Name],
>
> Attached: our top-12 Amazon outdoor knives list (FOB + landed estimate to LA).
>
> On the 3.5" liner-lock G10 black blade: you're likely looking at our **KL-OD-SS-012** (mirror-polish, G10, 3.5" blade). Before I quote, two quick checks so we're comparing the same knife:
>
> 1. **Blade steel**: Ningbo's $2.10 is likely 3Cr13 at HRC 52–54. Ours is 5Cr15MoV at **HRC 56–58**, SGS-tested. On Amazon, "dulls after a month" reviews kill a listing — edge retention is your #1 return driver.
> 2. **Liner lock mechanism**: ours is double-steel liner lock with ceramic ball detent (snappy open, no play). Many $2 knives use a cheap leaf spring that loosens after 200 opens.
>
> My indicative price for 1,000 pcs KL-OD-SS-012 with FBA prep + Prop 65 label: **$2.05 FOB Shenzhen** (example pricing, not our official quote yet). If you can move to 2,000 pcs across 2 colors, I can hit **$1.85**.
>
> Want a free sample to compare against your Ningbo knife? I'll ship it tomorrow.
>
> — Leo

**策略分析：**
- **不直接降价，先摆规格差异**（`09_行业知识与培训/15_B2B谈判技巧与异议处理.md` 心法 1）：客户说 $2.10，我们不否定，而是问"你那把是什么钢、什么锁"，把比较维度从"单价"拉到"HRC/锁机构/退货率"。
- **锚定 Amazon 卖家痛点**：退货率、差评——这是 Amazon 卖家的命门，比单价 $0.25 重要 10 倍。
- **阶梯价**：1000pcs $2.05 / 2000pcs $1.85，引导客户加量。
- **主动提免费样品**：美国客户决策快，样品一发就能测。
- ⚠️ 价格标注"example pricing"，避免后续被锁死。

---

### Round 4 — 客户接受样品（当天回）

**客户回件：**

> OK send the sample. Address: [Warehouse Address, LA]. 
>
> One question — your LA warehouse: what SKUs do you stock there? If I can get a restock in 7 days instead of 35, that changes my whole reorder strategy.
>
> — [Client Name]

**策略分析：**
- **客户兴趣点转移**：从"单价"转向"LA 仓现货 SKU"——说明客户真的在算补货模型。这是**强购买信号**。
- 回答这个问题时要具体：列 3–5 个 SKU + 现货数量，别写"we have many SKUs"。

---

### Round 5 — 我方寄样 + 列 LA 仓现货

**我方回件：**

> Hi [Client Name],
>
> Sample going out via FedEx today (tracking: [tracking], arrives Thu).
>
> **LA warehouse current stock** (ready to ship in 7 days to FBA):
>
> | SKU | Description | Stock | FBA landed cost/pc |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | KL-OD-SS-012 | G10 mirror folding knife, black blade | 800 pcs | $3.40 |
> | KL-OD-SS-004 | Rosewood folding hunter | 500 pcs | $3.60 |
> | KL-OD-TI-004 | All-black tactical fixed blade | 600 pcs | $4.10 |
> | KL-SC-PR-006 | Mirror-polish kitchen shears (cross-sell) | 1,000 pcs | $2.90 |
>
> LA stock pricing is higher than FOB (includes ocean freight + duty + our warehousing), but for restocks under 500 pcs it beats air freight and avoids stock-outs. You can mix FOB bulk orders with LA top-ups.
>
> Test the sample this week. I'll follow up Friday.
>
> — Leo

**策略分析：**
- **给具体数字**：4 个 SKU + 现货数 + 到货成本表，美国客户要的就是这种 spreadsheet 式回答。
- **商业模式解释清楚**：LA 仓不是最便宜，是**避免断货**。把客户的心智从"比单价"引导到"补货总成本模型"。
- **交叉销售**：顺手列了 KL-SC-PR-006 厨房剪（Amazon 卖家经常刀剪一起铺货）。
- **主动设跟进节点**："I'll follow up Friday"——美国客户喜欢有节奏的跟进，不讨厌被催。

---

### Round 6 — 样品测试后反馈（4 天后）

**客户回件：**

> Leo,
>
> Sample is good — edge is sharper than our Ningbo one, lock feels solid. Price at 2000pcs for $1.85 works.
>
> But: can you do the first order at 1000 pcs? I don't want to tie up $3,700 on a new supplier until I see your production QC. If the first batch is good, Q2 reorder will be 2000+.
>
> Also — FBA prep: do you do polybag suffocation warning + FNSKU label? Pictures of your prep process needed.
>
> — [Client Name]

**策略分析：**
- **客户已基本接受**，只剩 MOQ 和 QC 两个顾虑。
- "first order 1000, reorder 2000+"是典型美国 Amazon 卖家试单逻辑——**库存风险厌恶**。不要硬推 2000。
- FBA prep 流程照片是 Amazon 卖家的硬需求，必须给。

---

### Round 7 — 我方折中（1000pcs 按中间价 + 产前视频）

**我方回件：**

> Hi [Client Name],
>
> Fair — first order should be low-risk. Deal:
>
> - **1,000 pcs KL-OD-SS-012 at $1.95 FOB** (between the $2.05 / $1.85 tiers)
> - **Free FBA prep**: polybag + suffocation warning (English) + FNSKU label + carton marking
> - **Pre-production QC video** before we run the batch — you approve, then we ship
> - **30% T/T deposit + 70% before shipment**
> - Lead time 25 days after deposit
>
> Attached: 3 photos of our FBA prep station (polybag machine, label gun, carton marking).
>
> On the 2,000 pcs Q2 reorder — I'll lock the $1.85 price for 90 days so you're not exposed to steel price rises.
>
> Send the PI?
>
> — Leo

**策略分析：**
- **让步有条件**：不直接答应 1000pcs $1.85（那是 2000 档的价），而是取中间 $1.95。让步幅度小，但给了三样东西换信任：免费 FBA prep、产前 QC 视频、90 天锁价。
- **产前视频**是打消"新供应商 QC 不放心"的标准动作。
- **90 天锁价**给客户 Q2 加量的确定性，引导他后续加单。

---

### Round 8 — 客户下首单（2 天后）

**客户回件：**

> OK. Send PI. Wire will go out tomorrow.
>
> One more — can you ship the first 200 pcs from LA warehouse in 7 days? I'm running low on my current listing. The remaining 800 can come by sea in 25 days. That way I don't stock out.
>
> — [Client Name]

**策略分析：**
- **客户提了分批发货**——这是 LA 仓卖点的真正变现。美国客户精于库存周转，这个要求合理且对我方有利（LA 仓现货利润更高）。
- 答应分批发，但要把两批的价格/运费拆清楚，别糊在一起。

---

### Round 9 — 我方确认 PI（含分批发货条款）

**我方回件：**

> Hi [Client Name],
>
> Smart move — partial shipment solves your stock-out. Updated PI:
>
> **PI No. KLC-PI-2026-088**
>
> | Batch | Source | Qty | Unit Price | Notes |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | Batch 1 | LA warehouse stock | 200 pcs | $3.40 landed (includes FBA prep) | Ships in 7 days to FBA |
> | Batch 2 | FOB Shenzhen bulk | 800 pcs | $1.95 FOB | Ships in 25 days after deposit |
> | | | **Total** | Batch 1 $680 + Batch 2 $1,560 = **$2,240** | |
>
> Deposit: 30% of total = $672. Balance $1,568: $408 on Batch 1 pickup, $1,160 before Batch 2 ships.
>
> Sign and I'll release LA stock tomorrow.
>
> — Leo

**策略分析：**
- **分批发货拆分清楚**：LA 仓那批按落地价 $3.40，FOB 那批按 $1.95，两批价格逻辑不同但都讲得通。
- **付款也拆分**：让客户觉得每一批的钱对应每一批货，风险小。
- 整个对话 9 轮，从冷邮件到 PI 约 12 天——符合美国客户 1–2 周决策周期。

### 场景一复盘要点

| 维度 | 做法 |
|---|---|
| 切入点 | 不卖便宜货，卖"不缺货"——LA 仓 7 天补 FBA |
| 谈判 | 不直接降价，用 HRC/锁机构/退货率拉维度；量换价 |
| 信任 | 产前 QC 视频 + FBA prep 照片 + 分批发货 |
| 红线 | 不卖自动刀/弹簧刀，主动声明 |
| 节奏 | 邮件 24h 内回，短句，表格化，不寒暄 |

## 场景二：德国客户 — 厨刀进口商（LFGB 合规导向）

### 客户背景

| 项 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 国家 | 德国（法兰克福） |
| 公司 | Client B，中型厨刀进口商，约 40 人，分销给德国连锁厨房用品店 + 电商 |
| 采购需求 | 8 寸主厨刀，替换现有浙江供应商，作为第二货源 |
| 关注点 | **LFGB 认证、HRC 硬度、批次一致性、交期稳定、合同严谨** |
| 决策人 | Herr Bauer（采购经理），技术出身，会逐字看规格表 |
| 时区 | 法兰克福 CEST，北京 15:00–19:00 发信最佳 |

> **匹配产品**：KL-KN-SS-012（8" 主厨刀 5Cr15MoV HRC56-58 黑 Pakkawood 全龙骨）、KL-KN-SS-016（1.4116 锻造主厨刀）。
> （与剧本 01 的德国客户是不同客户，本例重点展示"沟通严谨、资料先行"的德国风格。）

---

### Round 1 — 开发信（北京时间 16:00，德国 10:00）

**我方发件：**

> **Subject: Second-source 8" chef knife for [Client B] — SGS LFGB certified, Yangjiang factory direct**
>
> Dear Mr. Bauer,
>
> According to customs records, [Client B] has been importing 8" chef knives from Zhejiang. We are KaiLionCrafts, a direct cutlery supply chain in Yangjiang (75% of China's knives come from here).
>
> We approach you as a potential **second source** with:
> - SGS LFGB food-contact certification (valid until 2028) + FDA + CE
> - Blade hardness **HRC 56–58**, third-party lab tested per batch
> - Full-tang construction, 15° double-bevel edge, CATRA ICP measured
> - EN 10204-3.1 mill test certificate available on request
> - 30% T/T deposit + 70% before shipment; L/C at sight possible for first order
>
> Attached: product catalog + SGS LFGB report + HRC test report. Would you be open to receiving samples for your QC lab?
>
> Mit freundlichen Grüßen,
> Leo Li
> KaiLionCrafts

**策略分析：**
- **称呼用 Dear Mr. Bauer**，不用 Hi——德国客户正式、重礼节（`04_国家地区开发指南/02_欧洲_德英法意西荷.md`）。
- **标题写全**：产品名 + 认证 + 产地，德国客户邮件标题要规范。
- **认证一次给全**：LFGB/FDA/CE/HRC/EN 10204-3.1——德国客户要"超详细资料包"，别等他要才给。
- **结尾用 Mit freundlichen Grüßen**（德语"此致"），小细节拉好感。

---

### Round 2 — 客户回复（3 天后，直接问技术问题）

**客户回件：**

> Dear Mr. Li,
>
> Thank you for the documentation. Before we proceed, please answer:
>
> 1. Your LFGB report is dated 2024-03. Is the retest scheduled? We require a report no older than 12 months.
> 2. The HRC report shows 56.5–57.8 — is this guaranteed per production batch, or only on the sample batch?
> 3. What is your defect rate on chef knives over the last 12 months? Please provide a quantified number, not a percentage statement.
> 4. Do you have BSCI or Sedex audit? Our retail partners require it.
>
> — regards, Bauer

**策略分析：**
- **德国客户的典型问法**：每个问题都带具体时间/数字要求，4 个问题全是技术+合规细节。
- ⚠️ **不能糊弄**：德国客户会对照附件逐字查。报告日期过期就老实说，不能编。
- BSCI 我们是 C 评级（剧本 05 已注明），被问起要**坦诚说明整改计划**，不假装 A 级。

---

### Round 3 — 我方逐条回答（不躲不编）

**我方回件：**

> Dear Mr. Bauer,
>
> Thank you for the precise questions. Answers point by point:
>
> 1. **LFGB retest**: The 2024-03 report is valid (LFGB is valid 3 years). We have scheduled the retest with SGS for **2026-11-15**; the new report will be issued within 10 working days. I will send it to you the moment it arrives.
> 2. **HRC guarantee**: The 56.5–57.8 figure is from the last production batch. For your order, we guarantee **HRC 56–58 on every batch**, with a random sample HRC test from each shipment included in the QC pack. We can also accept a third-party inspection (SGS/Bureau Veritas) at your cost before shipment.
> 3. **Defect rate**: On chef knives over the last 12 months across all customers, our rejected rate was **0.4%** (manufacturing defects: edge chipping, handle alignment, finish). We replace 100% of confirmed defects free of charge.
> 4. **Social audit**: We hold a **BSCI rating C** (valid to 2026-12). We acknowledge this is not yet rating B. We have a 6-month improvement plan (working hours documentation, fire safety drill records, dormitory audit) and expect to reach rating B by Q3 2027. The audit report is attached.
>
> If these answers are acceptable, I will send sample PI for two knives (KL-KN-SS-012 and KL-KN-SS-016).
>
> Mit freundlichen Grüßen,
> Leo

**策略分析：**
- **逐条编号回答**，德国客户最欣赏这种格式。
- **LFGB 主动说重测时间**，不装。
- **缺陷率给具体数字 0.4%**，不说"very low"。
- **BSCI C 级坦诚承认 + 给整改计划**——德国客户尊重诚实，反感包装。藏着掖着被发现就直接出局。
- 这封邮件的核心是"可验证、可追责"，不是"我们什么都好"。

---

### Round 4 — 客户接受样品（2 天后）

**客户回件：**

> Dear Mr. Li,
>
> The answers are satisfactory. Send the two samples. We will test edge retention and corrosion in our lab.
>
> One additional requirement: all knives for the German market must have the **steel grade and country of origin laser-etched on the blade bolster** (not on the packaging). Please confirm this is doable.
>
> — Bauer

**策略分析：**
- 客户提了一个新的合规细节：刀颈处钢印+产地。这是德国进口法规要求，不是客户刁难。
- 要立刻确认可做，并把这个要求记进 PI（避免大货时漏）。

---

### Round 5–6 — 样品寄出 + 客户实验室反馈（略）

> （流程同剧本 01 Round 12–14：样品 PI ×1.2 倍率，DHL 法兰克福，客户实验室测 HRC/CATRA/盐雾/手柄拉力，全部 pass。）

### Round 7 — 报价谈判

**客户回件：**

> Dear Mr. Li,
>
> Samples passed. Our target: 1,000 pcs KL-KN-SS-012 with laser logo + color box, at **€6.20 FOB Shenzhen** per piece. Your current quote is €7.10. Our existing Zhejiang supplier is at €6.40.
>
> We would prefer to consolidate with you given the better documentation. But €6.20 is our buyer-approved ceiling.
>
> — Bauer

**策略分析：**
- 德国客户报价用**欧元**（他采购预算是欧元），给的是 buyer-approved 内部审批价——说明他真有预算权限。
- 差距 €0.90（约 13%），不是天文数字。

---

### Round 8 — 我方不裸降，给"量+服务"组合

**我方回件：**

> Dear Mr. Bauer,
>

### Round 3 — 我方回复（DDP 估价 + 付款方案）

**我方回件：**

> Hi Carlos,
>
> Great questions. Let me break it down:
>
> **1. FOB price (500 pcs KL-KN-SS-012, 8" chef knife, Pakkawood handle):**
> - Unit FOB Shenzhen: **$6.80** (example)
> - 500 pcs = $3,400 FOB
>
> **2. Estimated DDP to São Paulo (CEP 01310-000):**
> - Ocean freight LCL: ~$1.20/pc
> - Brazilian import duty + ICMS + IPI: ~80%–110% of CIF value (varies by NCM code; we'll confirm with our broker)
> - Brokerage + warehouse to door: ~$1.50/pc
> - **Estimated landed: ~$14.50–$16.00/pc DDP São Paulo** (example, will confirm with broker before PI)
>
> I'll ask our Brazilian customs broker to lock the exact duty before we send the PI.
>
> **3. Payment — I understand the concern.** For first order we suggest:
> - **50% T/T deposit** before production
> - **50% against copy of Bill of Lading** (you see the goods actually shipped before paying balance)
> - We can also share our company bank reference, and we are covered by **Sinosure (China Export & Credit Insurance Corporation)** — you can verify with your bank.
>
> No OA, no 100% advance. This is fair for both sides.
>
> — Leo

**策略分析：**
- **DDP 给区间不给死数**：巴西关税按 NCM 编码浮动，不能瞎报死价，承诺"找报关行确认"——显专业。
- **50/50 付款结构**：比标准 30/70 对客户更友好（定金高一点，但见提单副本付尾款），同时我方风险可控。
- **提中信保**：巴西客户怕跨境，中信保是第三方背书，比"trust me"管用。
- **不做 100% 预付，也不做 OA**——两个极端都拒绝。

**我方回件：**

### Round 6–8 — 付款与下单（节奏慢，约 3–4 周）

> 巴西客户会反复确认银行信息、要公司注册文件、问能不能用 Boleto。我方坚持 T/T（Boleto 不现实），但提供：
> - 公司营业执照+进出口权扫描件
> - 银行 SWIFT 信息纸
> - 中信保保单编号可查
> - 分批发货：200pcs 试单走完，再谈 500–1000pcs 复购

**Round 8 最终 PI 结构（示例）：**

> - 200 pcs KL-KN-SS-012, DDP São Paulo
> - Unit landed: $16.50 → Total $3,300
> - Payment: 50% T/T deposit ($1,650) → production; 50% ($1,650) against B/L copy before our broker releases customs
> - Lead time: 35 days production + 35 days sea + customs 7 days = ~77 days to door
> - Portuguese label proof approved before printing
