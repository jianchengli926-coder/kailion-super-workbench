# 邮件营销素材库 (Email Marketing Material Library)

> 用途：B2B外贸场景下的邮件营销模板集。所有邮件为英文正文，括号 `[BRACKETS]` 为替换变量。
> 发送渠道：公司企业邮箱 ceo@kailioncrafts.com（Zoho Mail），配合独立站 newsletter 订阅与询盘自动回复。
> 发送纪律：纯文本优先、5-8行正文、300-500字符；HTML 须有纯文本降级；签名 ≤10000字符。

---

## 一、欢迎邮件（Welcome Series）

### E-WELCOME-01: 新订阅欢迎邮件（Newsletter Subscriber）

- **发送时机**：用户在独立站订阅框输入邮箱后 **即时触发（0分钟）**
- **目标**：确认订阅、建立第一印象、引导浏览核心品类

**Subject Line（A/B 测试备选）：**
- A: `Welcome to KaiLionCrafts — Your Cutlery Sourcing Partner`
- B: `Thanks for subscribing. Here's what we make.`

**Preview Text:** Yangjiang factory · OEM/ODM · Low MOQ · US warehouse stock

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

Thanks for subscribing to KaiLionCrafts.

We're a Yangjiang-based cutlery manufacturer — kitchen knives, professional scissors, outdoor blades, and BBQ tools — working with brands in 30+ countries.

Here's what you can expect from our newsletter:
  · New product drops and factory behind-the-scenes
  · Sourcing tips for private label knife brands
  · US warehouse restock announcements

Start browsing:
  → Kitchen Knives: [link]
  → Professional Scissors: [link]
  → Outdoor Knives: [link]
  → BBQ & Kitchen Tools: [link]

Want pricing instead of browsing? Reply to this email with your target product and quantity — we'll send a spec sheet within 24 hours.

Best,
Leo Li
KaiLionCrafts | Yangjiang, China
ceo@kailioncrafts.com
[Website] | [LinkedIn] | [Instagram]
```

### E-WELCOME-02: 新询盘欢迎邮件（New Inquiry / RFQ）

- **发送时机**：客户通过独立站询盘表单提交后 **即时自动回复（0分钟）**，随后人工 24 小时内跟进
- **目标**：确认收到、管理预期、引导补充信息以加速报价

**Subject Line:** `We received your inquiry — KaiLionCrafts`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

Thanks for reaching out to KaiLionCrafts. We've received your inquiry and our team is reviewing it now.

To get you a precise quote as fast as possible, it helps if you can reply with:

  1. Which product category are you interested in? (Kitchen knives / Scissors / Outdoor / BBQ tools)
  2. Approximate order quantity? (MOQ starts at 30-100 pcs depending on SKU)
  3. Do you need custom branding? (Logo engraving / custom packaging / OEM design)
  4. Target market / destination country? (We'll confirm compliance and shipping)

A dedicated account manager will follow up within 24 business hours.

In the meantime, you might find these resources useful:
  → Our catalog: [link]
  → US warehouse ready stock: [link]
  → OEM/ODM process guide: [link]

Best regards,
Leo Li
KaiLionCrafts
```

## 二、弃购挽回邮件序列（Abandoned Cart — 3封）

> 适用场景：独立站 B2B 客户把产品加入询盘车/报价单但未提交，或下载目录后未回复。

### E-ABANDON-01: 第一封 — 温和提醒（提交后 1 小时）

- **发送时机**：弃购后 **1 小时**
- **目标**：提醒未完成的动作，提供直接帮助入口

**Subject Line:** `You left something behind — [Product Name]?`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

We noticed you were looking at [Product Name / Category] on our website.

Did you have a question about specs, MOQ, or pricing? We're happy to help.

Here's a quick summary of what you were viewing:
  · Product: [Product Name] ([SKU])
  · Steel / Material: [Material]
  · MOQ: [MOQ] pieces
  · Unit price range: [Price Range]

Reply to this email and we'll send you the full spec sheet and wholesale quote — no obligation.

Best,
Leo Li
KaiLionCrafts
```

---

### E-ABANDON-02: 第二封 — 价值补充（弃购后 24 小时）

- **发送时机**：第一封后 **24 小时**
- **目标**：用额外价值（免费样品/目录/案例）重新打开对话

**Subject Line:** `Still deciding? Here's our 2026 catalog.`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

Following up on your earlier visit — wanted to make sure you have everything you need.

Attached is our latest product catalog with:
  · Full SKU list across 4 categories
  · Steel grade and HRC specs
  · MOQ and FOB pricing tiers
  · US warehouse ready-stock items

A few things buyers ask us about most:
  ✓ Samples available before bulk order
  ✓ Custom logo laser engraving (setup fee waived on first order over [X] pcs)
  ✓ FBA labeling and packaging handled in-house

Want a sample shipped to your address? Just reply with your courier account number or shipping address.

Best,
Leo Li
```

---

### E-ABANDON-03: 第三封 — 最后一封（弃购后 72 小时）

- **发送时机**：第二封后 **48 小时**（即弃购后 72 小时）
- **目标**：最后一次触达，降低回复门槛，关闭序列

**Subject Line:** `Last note — should we close your inquiry?`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

We haven't heard back, so I'll assume the timing isn't right. No problem at all.

If you'd like to keep the conversation open, just reply with "yes" and we'll send over pricing. Otherwise, we'll close this inquiry and you won't hear from us again about this product.

Either way, thanks for visiting KaiLionCrafts. We're a Yangjiang factory that's been making cutlery for [X]+ years, and we'd love to work with you when the time is right.

Wishing you a great week,
Leo Li
KaiLionCrafts | ceo@kailioncrafts.com
```

## 五、客户复购激活邮件（Win-Back / Reactivation）

### E-WINBACK-01: 沉睡客户激活（90天未下单）

- **发送时机**：客户最后一次互动后 **90 天**
- **目标**：重新激活沉默客户，推送新品/库存变化

**Subject Line:** `We haven't talked in a while — new stock arrived`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

It's been a few months since your last order. We wanted to reach out because a few things have changed on our end:

  📦 New: [Product Category] line just hit the US warehouse — 1-3 day delivery
  🔧 Lower MOQ: we now accept trial orders from [X] pieces on selected SKUs
  🎁 New packaging: premium gift boxes now available as a free upgrade on orders over [X] pcs

If your sourcing needs have changed, or you're exploring new product categories, we'd love to hear what you're working on.

No hard sell — just reply and let us know what you need, or say "not now" and we'll close this thread.

Best,
Leo Li
KaiLionCrafts
```

---

### E-WINBACK-02: 老客户复购提醒（订单周期触发）

- **发送时机**：客户上一批订单预计售罄前 **30 天**（按历史订单量估算）
- **目标**：提醒补货，推动复购

**Subject Line:** `Time to restock? Your last order was [X] days ago`

**Body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

KaiLionCrafts
```

> **发送纪律提醒**（来自 `07_邮件营销与多账号防封禁.md`）：
> - 新邮箱前 2-4 周每天发信 ≤20-50 封，逐步加量
> - 签名纯文本 5-8 行 / 300-500 字符，Logo 内嵌小图 ≤50KB
> - 必须配 SPF/DKIM/DMARC，数据中心选美国
> - 只发给已授权联系人，不群发未授权列表
