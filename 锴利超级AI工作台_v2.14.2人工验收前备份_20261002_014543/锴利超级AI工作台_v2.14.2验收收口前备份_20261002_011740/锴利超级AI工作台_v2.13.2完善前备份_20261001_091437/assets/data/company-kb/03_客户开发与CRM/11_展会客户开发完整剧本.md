# 实战剧本11：展会客户开发完整剧本

> **用途**：把一次展会从"去摆摊"变成"有计划的线索收割战役"。覆盖展前 30 天邀约 → 展中接待探需 → 展后 24h/72h/1周/2周/1月跟进全链路。
> **适用展会**：广交会（Canton Fair）、法兰克福 Ambiente、芝加哥 Housewares Show、东京 Gift Show 等。
> **与其他剧本关系**：展后跟进接到线索后，转入 `03_客户开发与CRM/实战剧本库/01_新客户完整开发链路剧本.md` 和 `03_客户开发与CRM/实战剧本库/05_大客户深度开发剧本.md`。
> ⚠️ 所有价格/MOQ/折扣/数量为示例数据，非公司实际定价。客户一律用 Client A/B/C 代称。

---

## 一、展会战役总览

```
展前 T-30 ──→ T-14 ──→ T-7 ──→ T-1 ──→ 展中 5天 ──→ 展后 24h / 72h / 1周 / 2周 / 1月
  邀约邮件     提醒       最后邀请    展前一天    接待/探需/留资/分级    跟进序列
```

**展前目标**：让已知/潜在客户知道你在哪、约好见面时间。
**展中目标**：接待 ≥ X 组客户，每组留资并当场分级。
**展后目标**：24h 内全部发感谢信，72h 内发资料包，1 周内出报价。

## 二、展前邀约邮件序列（T-30 / T-14 / T-7 / T-1）

### 2.1 T-30 首次邀约（英文模板）

**Subject**：Let's meet at [展会名] — Booth #[展位号]

```
Hi [Client A name],

Will you be attending [Canton Fair / Ambiente Frankfurt] this [October / February]?

We'll be exhibiting at Booth #[展位号], showcasing our latest:
- 8-inch German 1.4116 chef knives
- Premium kitchen shears (take-apart, dishwasher-safe)
- Damascus folding knives for outdoor/EDC

We'd love to meet you in person. Would a quick 15-minute chat at our booth work for you?
You can book a slot here: [Calendly/日历链接]

Looking forward to seeing you there.

Best regards,
Leo Li
KaiLionCrafts | Yangjiang, China
```

### 2.2 T-14 提醒（英文模板）

**Subject**：Reminder: Catch us at Booth #[展位号]

```
Hi [Client A name],

Just a quick reminder that [展会名] is in 2 weeks.

Come visit us at Booth #[展位号]. We'll have live demos — sharpness tests, Damascus cutting shows, and a factory tour video playing at the booth.

If you'd like to see a specific SKU or discuss OEM/ODM, let me know in advance and I'll have it ready for you.

See you soon.
Leo
```

### 2.3 T-7 最后邀请（英文模板）

**Subject**：One week to go — Booth #[展位号]

```
Hi [Client A name],

One week until [展会名]. The early-bird meeting slots are filling up fast.

If you're coming, reply with a day/time that works, and I'll save you a seat. Even a 10-minute walk-by is great — I'll have samples and new catalogs ready.

Booth #[展位号]. See you there.
Leo
```

### 2.4 T-1 展前一天（WhatsApp/短信，短）

```
Hi [Client A name], tomorrow's the day! Booth #[展位号] — come say hi. I'll have your requested samples ready. See you! 🤝
```

## 三、展中接待剧本（开场 → 探需 → 演示 → 留资 → 分级）

### 3.1 接待五步法

| 步骤 | 时间 | 动作 | 目的 |
|:-:|---|---|---|
| 1. 开场 | 10s | 微笑、递名片、问来源国 | 破冰、判断市场 |
| 2. 探需 | 2–3min | 问 3 个问题（见下） | 判断 ABCD 级 |
| 3. 演示 | 2–3min | 针对性演示他感兴趣的 SKU | 勾起兴趣 |
| 4. 留资 | 1min | 扫名片/填名片/留 WhatsApp | 拿到联系方式 |
| 5. 分级 | 30s | 当场在名片上标注 ABCD | 展后优先跟进 |

### 3.2 开场英文对话（Opening）

> **You**: Hi there! Welcome to KaiLionCrafts. Where are you coming from?
> **Client A**: Germany.
> **You**: Great to meet you! I'm Leo. Are you a distributor, retailer, or brand owner?
> **Client A**: We're a kitchenware distributor based in Stuttgart.

### 3.3 探需三问（Qualifying · 英文）

1. "What products are you currently sourcing in this category?"
2. "What's your typical order quantity — and what's your main market?"
3. "Are you looking for stock items, or do you need OEM/private label?"

> 这三问直接决定分级：
> - A 级：品牌方/大经销商，有明确数量，要 OEM → 展后 24h 重点跟进
> - B 级：中型零售商，现货采购，量中等 → 展后 72h 跟进
> - C 级：小批发/Amazon 卖家，量小 → 展后 1 周跟进
> - D 级：同行/学生/纯看热闹 → 不跟进

### 3.4 演示英文话术（Demo · 按客户类型）

**对德国/欧洲客户（重品质）**：
> "Feel free to pick it up. This is German 1.4116 steel, HRC 56–58. Watch the tomato test — no juice, no bruising. Every blade passes 7 QC checks before it leaves the factory."

**对美国客户（重性价比/现货）**：
> "We keep bestsellers in our US warehouse — 3–5 day delivery within the States. MOQ for stock items is [示例: 500 pcs]. For private label, MOQ is [示例: 1,000 pcs]."

**对中东/拉美客户（重价格）**：
> "We have different steel grades at different price points. Let me show you the value line — same handle design, 3Cr13 steel, much better margin for you."

### 3.5 留资与分级（Lead Capture）

- 当场在客户名片背面用记号笔标注：**A/B/C + 国家 + 感兴趣的 SKU + 约定下一步**。
- 示例标注：`A | DE | chef knife 8" | wants quote after show`
- 当天闭馆后立刻录入 CRM：`03_客户开发与CRM/16_CRM字段定义与数据规范.md`。

## 四、展后跟进邮件序列（24h / 72h / 1周 / 2周 / 1月）

### 4.1 展后 24 小时（感谢信 · 全部客户）

**Subject**：Great meeting you at Booth #[展位号] — KaiLionCrafts

```
Hi [Client A name],

It was great meeting you at [展会名] yesterday. Thank you for stopping by Booth #[展位号].

As promised, I've attached:
1. Our company profile & catalog
2. The SKUs you were interested in: [chef knife 8", kitchen shears]
3. Our latest QC certificate

Could you confirm your email and tell me:
- Target order quantity?
- Do you need private label / custom packaging?

I'll prepare a formal quotation within 48 hours after your reply.

Best regards,
Leo Li
KaiLionCrafts
```

### 4.2 展后 72 小时（发资料包/报价 · A/B 级客户）

**Subject**：Quotation for [SKUs] — as discussed

```
Hi [Client A name],

Following up from the show. Here's the quotation we discussed:

| SKU | Description | MOQ | Unit Price (FOB) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [示例] KL-CK8 | 8" Chef Knife, 1.4116 | 500 pcs | $[示例: 8.50] |
| [示例] KL-KS01 | Take-apart Kitchen Shears | 500 pcs | $[示例: 3.20] |

*Prices are FOB Yangjiang, for reference only (示例数据).*

Sample lead time: 7 days. Bulk lead time: 35–40 days.

Shall I send the sample? I can have it out this week.

Leo
```

### 4.3 展后 1 周（促样品/促决策）

```
Hi [Client A name],

Just checking in — did you receive the quotation?

I understand you're comparing suppliers. To make it easier, I'd like to offer:
- Free sample (you only cover shipping)
- A short factory video call so you can see the production line

Would a 15-minute video call next week work? I'm flexible on time zones.

Leo
```

### 4.4 展后 2 周（最后一推 · 仍未回复）

```
Hi [Client A name],

I don't want to clutter your inbox, so this will be my last follow-up on this show lead.

If now isn't the right time, no worries at all. If you'd still like samples or a revised quote, just reply "yes" and I'll get it going.

Either way, thanks again for visiting our booth.

Leo
```

### 4.5 展后 1 月（长线孵化 · 未成交客户）

```
Hi [Client A name],

It's been a month since [展会名]. Hope business is going well.

We just launched [new SKU / new finish] and thought of you. Here's a 30-second demo: [video link].

No pressure — just wanted to keep you in the loop. We'd love to work together someday.

Leo
```

## 五、展会客户分级与转化跟踪

| 级别 | 特征 | 跟进节奏 | 目标 |
|:-:|---|---|---|
| A | 品牌方/大经销商，明确数量+OEM | 24h 邮件 + 48h 报价 + 1 周内视频会议 | 样品单 → 首单 |
| B | 中型零售商，现货采购 | 72h 报价 + 2 周内跟进 | 样品单 |
| C | 小批发/Amazon 卖家 | 1 周后批量邮件 | 留长线 |
| D | 同行/看热闹 | 不跟进 | — |

**转化漏斗跟踪**（录入 `18_询盘管理与转化漏斗.md`）：
`接待客户数 → 留资数 → 寄样数 → 报价数 → 成交数`
