## 二、按锁定机制对比（Locking Mechanisms）

> ⚠️ 本库折刀具体锁定方式逐 SKU **待工厂确认**；以下为类型对比，供客户咨询时参考。

| 锁定机制 | 优点 | 缺点 | 法规敏感度 | 适合场景 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Liner Lock 衬锁** | 结构简单、单手快开、成本低 | 长期使用可能松动 | 中（欧洲单手开受限） | EDC 折刀主流（本库多数 SS/TI 折刀） |
| **Frame Lock 框架锁** | 强度高、手感干脆 | 制造精度要求高 | 中 | 高端折刀、钛柄款 |
| **Axis Lock** | 居中开锁、双手均可操作、手感顺滑 | 结构复杂、成本高 | 中 | 高端 EDC（⚠️ Axis Lock 是注册商标，须大写） |
| **Back Lock 背锁** | 经典可靠、强度高 | 开锁声音大、手感偏硬 | 中低 | 传统折刀、猎刀折刀 |
| **无锁（Slip Joint）** | 无锁=在英国/日本合法口袋刀 | 不能承重用力 | **低**（英国/日本唯一合法折刀） | 英国/日本市场刚需 |

> **给客户建议**：
> - 客户在**英国**→ 必须无锁 + 刃长 ≤3"（7.62cm），否则是攻击性武器
> - 客户在**日本**→ 无锁折刀 <5.5cm；有锁 ≥5.5cm 需公安委员会许可
> - 客户在**德国/法国/意大利**→ 单手开启锁定折刀公共携带受限
> - 客户在**美国/澳大利亚**→ 手动单刃折刀正常进口

## 八、常见误区（Common Myths）

1. **"直刀一定比折刀好"**
   → 看场景。直刀强度高但必须佩鞘腰挂；折刀口袋即走。EDC 日常用折刀更实用；重型野外/bushcraft 才需要直刀。

2. **"HRC 越高户外刀越好"**
   → 错。野外环境需要韧性（抗冲击、不易崩口），高碳钢 1095（58–60 HRC）虽然不算最硬，但韧性好、石头上就能磨，是 bushcraft 首选。M390 虽然 61+ HRC 保持极强，但崩口了野外很难修。

3. **"高碳钢会锈所以不能买"**
   → 半对半错。高碳钢确实怕湿，但它**野外极易打磨**（bushcraft 核心诉求），且养出 patina 后反而更防锈。对会保养的客户（擦干+涂油），高碳钢是优势不是缺点。

4. **"自动刀/弹簧刀很酷，能不能发？"**
   → **绝对不行**。公司不生产、不发运任何自动刀/OTF/弹簧刀/蝴蝶刀/重力刀。这些在美/加/澳/日/英/欧盟几乎全部禁运。德州虽允许但我们也不做这个品类。

5. **"Karambit 爪刀只是游戏周边，没关系吧？"**
   → 法规敏感。Karambit 在多国被视为攻击性武器，**不推荐欧洲/日本 B2C**。它在 CS:GO 玩家中好卖，但发货前必须确认目的国合规。

6. **"Kydex 鞘不如真皮鞘"**
   → 看场景。Kydex 静音、抗摔、快拔、防水——战术/生存场景反而更好；真皮鞘传统美观但怕水。HC 猎刀配皮鞘，TI 战术配 Kydex，各有定位。

7. **"涂层黑刃只是为了好看"**
   → 错。黑钛/DLC/Black Oxide 涂层核心功能是**防反光（隐蔽）+ 抗腐蚀**。战术/狩猎场景不反光很重要，不是纯装饰。

8. **"进口到哪国都一样"**
   → 大错。户外刀是法规差异最大的品类：英国无锁 ≤3"、日本 ≥15cm 需许可、德国 >12cm 携带受限、中东 B2C 直邮禁运、法国 Zombie 风格管制加严。**每票必须让客户报关行书面确认**。

## 九、英文客户问答（English Customer Q&A）

**Q1: Fixed blade or folding knife — which should I choose?**
A: It depends on use. If you need a tool for heavy outdoor work — bushcraft, processing game, camping survival — go with a fixed blade (Full Tang, comes with a sheath). If you want something for everyday pocket carry, opening boxes, light tasks, a folding EDC knife is more practical. We recommend our KL-OD-HC-006 (hand-forged carbon steel fixed blade) for bushcraft, and KL-OD-SS-012 (G10 folder) for EDC.

**Q2: What steel is best for an outdoor knife?**
A: For field use where you may need to sharpen with a rock, high-carbon steel (1095-style, like our KL-OD-HC-006) is tough and easy to touch up. For edge retention without frequent sharpening, we offer M390 powder steel (KL-OD-HC-001, 61±1 HRC) or 440C stainless (our SS/TI EDC folders). Damascus (VG10 core) is for collectors and premium gifts.

**Q3: Can you ship knives to my country?**
A: This depends entirely on your country's laws. Please tell us: (1) destination country/state, (2) intended use, and (3) the specific SKU. We'll provide blade length, lock type, and edge configuration for your customs broker to confirm in writing. We do NOT ship automatic/OTF/switchblade or balisong knives to any market.

**Q4: What's the rule in the UK?**
A: In the UK, a folding pocket knife with a cutting edge of 3 inches (7.62 cm) or less AND no locking mechanism qualifies as a legal pocket knife. Any locked folding knife is treated as an offensive weapon. We do not recommend locked folders for UK B2C sales.

**Q5: What's the rule in Japan?**
A: Fixed blades 15 cm or longer require a Public Safety Committee permit. Locked folding knives with a blade of 5.5 cm or longer are classified as controlled blades. Automatic knives are fully prohibited. Japanese customers should choose non-locked folders under 5.5 cm, or confirm their permit status before ordering.

**Q6: Do you ship automatic / OTF / switchblade knives?**
A: No. We do not produce or ship automatic-opening, OTF, switchblade, gravity, or balisong/butterfly knives. These are prohibited for import in nearly all major markets.

**Q7: What sheath do your fixed blades come with?**
A: Our hunting fixed blades (HC series) come with a genuine leather sheath. Our tactical fixed blades (TI series) come with a Kydex sheath. Sheath options can be customized per order.

**Q8: How do I care for a high-carbon outdoor knife?**
A: High-carbon steel is not stainless — wipe the blade dry after every use and oil it before storage. A patina will develop over time; this is normal and actually protects the blade. Damascus blades also need regular drying and occasional oiling.
