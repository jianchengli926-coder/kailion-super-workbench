**Q: How do I maintain scissors to keep them sharp?**
**A:** Wipe blades dry after use, especially when cutting acidic or wet materials. Do not cut materials beyond the scissors' rated capacity (e.g., don't use kitchen shears to cut wire). Tighten the pivot screw if play develops. For professional hair scissors, use a professional sharpening service. Apply a light oil on the pivot periodically.
- 技术依据：行业通用维护知识；`专业剪刀/03_专业剪刀规格参数标准.md` 可调中心螺丝行
