**Q: Are your products food-grade / food-contact safe?**
**A:** Yes. All food-contact components are food-grade / food-contact safe. Stainless steel uses 18/10 or 304 grade. Silicone components are food-grade silicone. Our main factory holds SGS LFGB food-contact compliance and US FDA lead migration certification. SGS 21 heavy metal migration tests all returned ND (not detected).
- 技术依据：`厨房用品/03_厨房用品规格参数标准.md` 食品级标准节；`资质认证/02_产品认证详解.md` LFGB/FDA
- 关联SKU：KL-KA-CU-001, KL-KA-TG-001/002, KL-KA-GAD-020

**Q: What is the heat resistance of your silicone products?**
**A:** Food-grade silicone typically withstands -40°C to 230°C. However, exact temperature ratings are product-specific — please confirm against individual product test reports. Cast iron and enameled cast iron are oven-safe. Wood/bamboo items should avoid prolonged soaking and high-heat dishwashers.
- 技术依据：`厨房用品/03_厨房用品规格参数标准.md` 耐热温度表

**Q: Are wooden cutting boards dishwasher safe?**
**A:** No. Wood and bamboo cutting boards should be hand-washed and air-dried. Avoid prolonged soaking and high-heat dishwashers, which can cause warping, cracking, and splitting. Oil wooden boards periodically to maintain the surface.
- 技术依据：`厨房用品/03_厨房用品规格参数标准.md` 木/竹保养提示
