## 四、选购要点（Key Buying Criteria）

### 1. 耐温（Heat Resistance）
- **食品级硅胶**：-40°C ~ 230°C（446°F）——烤箱/BBQ 通用
- **304 不锈钢**：高温烹饪无压力
- **竹木**：❌ 不可高温洗碗机、不可长时间浸泡（会翘曲开裂）
- ⚠️ 逐 SKU 耐温以检测报告为准

### 2. 食品安全（Food Safety）
- 必须 FDA/LFGB 食品接触认证
- 不锈钢选 304（18/8），不是 201 仿品（201 易生锈、耐酸差）
- 硅胶必须食品级，不可用工业硅胶
- 竹木需 FSC 认证+食品接触处理

### 3. 易清洁（Easy Clean）
- 硅胶/不锈钢：洗碗机友好（除竹木外）
- 压蒜器 GP-001：可拆卸清洗
- 可拆卸厨房剪：见剪刀指南
- ⚠️ 竹木/木砧板**必须手洗晾干、定期涂矿物油**

### 4. 耐用性（Durability）
- 端纹木砧板（CB-002）：自愈合表面，护刀，寿命长
- 304 不锈钢：比 201 防锈耐久
- 硅胶头：不会像金属那样刮伤锅，自身也耐老化
- 磁吸刀架 MK-001：免打孔、强力磁吸（承重待工厂确认）

## 六、常见误区（Common Myths）

1. **"所有不锈钢都一样"**
   → 错。304（18/8）食品级防锈耐酸；201 是便宜仿品，遇盐水/醋会锈。我们用 304，listing 要标清楚 "304 stainless, not 201 knockoffs"。

2. **"木砧板不如塑料砧板卫生"**
   → 错。研究表明端纹木砧板天然抑菌（木材本身有抗菌性），且自愈合护刀。塑料砧板虽然可洗碗机，但刀痕里反而藏菌。关键是保养——木砧板要手洗晾干涂油。

3. **"硅胶厨具会融化"**
   → 合格食品级硅胶耐 230°C（446°F），日常炒菜/烤箱 BBQ 不会融化。便宜工业硅胶才会。认准 FDA/LFGB 认证的食品级硅胶。

4. **"砧板可以洗碗机洗"**
   → 木/竹砧板**绝对不能洗碗机**——高温高压+长时间浸泡会翘曲开裂。只能手洗晾干。PP 塑料砧板（CB-003）可以洗碗机。

5. **"颜色砧板只是好看"**
   → 不是。三色分色（红=肉/蓝=鱼/绿=菜）是食品安全标准做法，防止生熟交叉污染。商业厨房强制分色，家庭也该养成习惯。

6. **"曼陀林切片器和普通切菜一样"**
   → 它快但危险。必须配护手器（handguard），销售时提示客户注意安全。8 刀头批量切薯片/胡萝卜丝是 meal-prep/TikTok 爆款。

7. **"磁性刀架不如刀盒安全"**
   → 对刀更好。刀盒装刀刀身在盒里互相碰撞会钝；磁吸刀架刀刃悬空不碰，且展示刀型、省抽屉空间。是买刀后的天然搭售品。

8. **"316 一定比 304 好"**
   → 看场景。316 加钼，耐海水/更强腐蚀，主要用于医疗/船用。日常厨房 304 完全够用且性价比高。没必要为厨房小件多付 316 溢价。

## 七、英文客户问答（English Customer Q&A）

**Q1: Are your kitchen accessories food-grade / food-contact safe?**
A: Yes. All food-contact components are food-grade. Stainless steel uses 304 (18/8) grade; silicone is food-grade. Our main factory holds SGS LFGB food-contact compliance and US FDA 21 CFR 109.6 lead-migration pass. SGS also tested 21 heavy metals — all ND (not detected) per EN 13130.

**Q2: Are your silicone utensils safe on non-stick pans?**
A: Absolutely. Silicone heads are soft by design — they won't scratch non-stick coatings. Our silicone utensil set (KL-KA-COOK-SU-001) and silicone-tipped tongs (KL-KA-COOK-TG-001) are specifically made for non-stick cookware.

**Q3: What temperature can your silicone products withstand?**
A: Food-grade silicone typically withstands -40°C to 230°C (-40°F to 446°F), which covers oven, BBQ, and stovetop use. Exact ratings are product-specific — please confirm against the individual test report.

**Q4: Can wooden / bamboo cutting boards go in the dishwasher?**
A: No. Wood and bamboo boards must be hand-washed and air-dried. Prolonged soaking and high-heat dishwashers cause warping, cracking, and splitting. Oil wooden boards periodically to maintain the surface. Our color-coded PP boards (CB-003) are dishwasher-safe.

**Q5: What's the difference between your bamboo and end-grain wood boards?**
A: The bamboo board (CB-001, FSC-certified) is an eco-friendly everyday board with a juice groove — great value. The end-grain butcher block (CB-002, walnut/maple) is a premium chef's piece: the end-grain surface is self-healing and protects your knife edges, making it the signature board in a serious kitchen.

**Q6: What's the MOQ? Can I mix products across categories?**
A: Mixed MOQ is 50–500 pcs depending on the item. Light customization (logo) starts at 50–150 pcs. Yes — we strongly encourage mixed container shipments across knives, scissors, outdoor knives, and kitchen accessories. This is ideal for buyers building a complete kitchen brand.

**Q7: Do you offer BBQ tool sets?**
A: Yes. We have an entry 6-piece 304 stainless set with carry bag (KL-KA-BBQ-TS-001, MOQ only 30) and a deluxe 21-piece aluminum-case gift set (KL-KA-SET-BBQ-001, $60–80 retail) — perfect for Father's Day gifting.

**Q8: Can you bundle kitchen accessories with knives and scissors?**
A: Yes. Mixed container orders across all four categories (kitchen knives, scissors, outdoor knives, kitchen accessories) are welcome. US warehouse stock can also be mixed with Yangjiang factory orders in a single shipment — the easiest way to build a full kitchen brand.
