# 质保与售后政策

> 独立站质保页已上线，以"四级质保体系"建立 B 端买家信任。
> 关联文件：`01_资质证书总览.md`、`02_产品认证详解.md`、`04_钢材材质证明.md`、`06_海外仓与订单物流/06_售后与索赔流程.md`、`06_海外仓与订单物流/07_售后服务与质保体系.md`。

## 一、四级质保体系

| 层级 | 承诺 | 英文 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 第一级 | **终身质保** | Lifetime Warranty | 产品材料与工艺问题终身质保 |
| 第二级 | **10 年刃口质保** | 10-Year Edge Retention Warranty | 刃口切割性能 10 年保障 |
| 第三级 | **2 年装配质保** | 2-Year Assembly Warranty | 手柄/刀身装配工艺 2 年保障 |
| 第四级 | **零缺陷标准** | Zero Defect Standard | 出厂零缺陷检验 |

## 二、信任支撑体系

质保承诺建立在第三方认证与工厂质控之上：

- 食品接触安全：SGS LFGB PASS、US FDA 铅迁移 PASS、21 项重金属 ND
- 材料合规：RoHS、CE、TÜV SÜD 测试报告
- 钢材溯源：Outokumpu EN 10204-3.1 材质证明
- 工厂质控：产前 QC 视频 + 创始人亲自审单

## 三、英文质保页面文案模板

```
KaiLionCrafts Four-Tier Warranty

At KaiLionCrafts, every knife we supply is backed by a four-tier warranty that reflects
the quality of Yangjiang manufacturing.

1. LIFETIME WARRANTY
   Every knife is warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for the lifetime
   of the original purchaser. If a knife fails due to manufacturing defect, we will repair
   or replace it free of charge.

2. 10-YEAR EDGE RETENTION WARRANTY
   Our blades are heat-treated to HRC 56-58. We guarantee the edge will hold its cutting
   performance for 10 years under normal use. If the edge dulls prematurely due to steel
   quality or heat-treatment issues, we will re-grind or replace the blade.

3. 2-YEAR ASSEMBLY WARRANTY
   Handles, rivets, and bolsters are guaranteed against loosening or separation for 2 years.

4. ZERO DEFECT STANDARD
   Every knife passes our pre-shipment inspection: blade straightness, edge sharpness,
   handle finish, and packaging integrity. We share QC photos/videos before shipment.

What's not covered:
- Normal wear and tear from use
- Accidental damage, misuse, or improper sharpening
- Knives that have been modified or repaired by the buyer
- Damage from dishwashers or corrosive environments
```

## 四、售后与沟通

| 项目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 客户对接 | 创始人直接对接海外客户（Leo Li 英语沟通） |
| 售后沟通 | 质量问题、补单、退换货沟通 |
| 物流 | 合作货代，EXW→海外仓全链路，协助售后物流 |
| 认证协助 | 协助客户办理 CE / LFGB / FDA 等认证文件 |

### 4.1 售后处理 SOP

| 问题类型 | 响应时间 | 处理方式 |
|---------|---------|---------|
| 运输破损 | 24 小时内 | 客户提供照片 → 补发 or 部分退款 |
| 刃口缺陷（工厂原因） | 48 小时内 | 下批货补发 or 折价 |
| 装配松动 | 48 小时内 | 指导客户紧固 or 补发 |
| 客户使用不当 | 72 小时内 | 提供保养建议，酌情补发 |
| 大货质量争议 | 5 个工作日 | 第三方检验（SGS/BV），按检验结果处理 |

### 4.2 客户索赔英文回复模板

```
Subject: Re: Quality Issue — Order #[XXX]

Hi [Customer Name],

Thank you for sending the photos and videos. We take product quality seriously.

We have reviewed the issue with our factory. [Brief explanation: e.g., "the blade tip
chip appears to have occurred during transit packaging, not during manufacturing."]

We propose the following solution:
- Option A: We will send [X] replacement units with your next order, at no charge.
- Option B: We will issue a [X%] credit note against your next purchase.

Please let us know which option you prefer, and we will process it within 2 business days.

We will also improve our packaging for this SKU to prevent recurrence.

Best regards,
Leo Li
KaiLionCrafts
```

## 五、B2B 质保与 B2C 的区别

> ⚠️ 我们是 B2B，质保条款与 Amazon 上的 B2C 品牌不同。

| 维度 | B2C 品牌（如 Dalstrong） | KaiLionCrafts B2B |
|------|------------------------|-------------------|
| 质保对象 | 终端消费者 | 采购商/品牌商 |
| 质保执行 | 品牌方直接换 | 通过采购商体系执行 |
| 终身质保 | 对消费者承诺 | 对工厂采购方承诺（大客户合同约定） |
| 索赔流程 | 在线提交即换 | 需照片/视频证据，工厂确认 |
| 不覆盖 | 正常磨损 | 同上 + 客户自行改装 |

> ⚠️ 具体质保条款的英文模板、免责范围、退换货比例等细节，以独立站质保页与客户合同为准；本文件为知识库摘要。
