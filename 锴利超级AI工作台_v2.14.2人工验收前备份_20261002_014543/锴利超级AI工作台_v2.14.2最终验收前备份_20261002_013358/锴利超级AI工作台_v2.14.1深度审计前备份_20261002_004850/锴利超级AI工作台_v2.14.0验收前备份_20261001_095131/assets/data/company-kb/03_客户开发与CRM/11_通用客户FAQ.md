**Q: What trade terms do you work with?**
**A:** EXW (Ex Works), FOB (Free On Board), CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight), and DDP (Delivered Duty Paid). FOB is most common for bulk ocean freight. DDP is ideal for buyers without import experience — we handle both customs clearance and delivery to your door.

**Q: How do you handle quality control?**
**A:** Three rounds of QC: (1) Incoming raw material inspection — steel hardness/composition/dimensions verified before production; (2) In-process inspection — 100% key dimension/function checks during production, with issues corrected on the production line within hours; (3) Founder pre-shipment confirmation — Leo personally reviews every batch, records a pre-shipment video (packaging, labels, sharpness, handle assembly), and ships only after your written approval. We also have 7 extreme tests at our partner lab (Rockwell HRC, edge angle, sharpness, dishwasher durability, salt spray, tensile).
- 技术依据：`06_海外仓与订单物流/07_售后服务与质保体系.md` 三轮QC节；`工厂与供应链/01_合作工厂体系.md` 汇达厂
