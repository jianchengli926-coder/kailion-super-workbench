# 实战剧本13：社媒私信开发剧本

> **用途**：在 LinkedIn / Instagram / Facebook 上通过私信（DM）开发 B2B/B2C 客户，而不是只靠邮件。
> **核心心法**：私信 = 社交场合搭话，不是发广告。先互动、再价值、后提案，**绝不一上来就甩链接和报价**。
> ⚠️ 所有价格/MOQ/数量为示例数据。客户用 Client A/B/C 代称。

---

## 一、社媒私信 vs 邮件：区别与适用场景

| 维度 | 邮件 Email | 社媒私信 DM |
|---|---|---|
| 打开率 | 冷邮件 10–25% | 私信 60–90%（推送提醒强） |
| 正式度 | 高，适合报价/合同 | 低，适合破冰/建立关系 |
| 回复速度 | 慢（天级） | 快（分钟/小时级） |
| 适合 | 报价、PI、长资料 | 找对人、破冰、预热 |
| 风险 | 进垃圾箱 | 频繁发送=封号 |
| 最佳组合 | DM 破冰 → 要邮箱 → 转邮件发资料 | — |

**结论**：私信负责"认识人"，邮件负责"谈生意"。DM 聊热了立刻要邮箱转正式沟通。

## 二、LinkedIn 私信开发剧本（B2B 主战场）

### 2.1 四步序列：连接请求 → 首次私信 → 价值提供 → 邀约通话

**Step 1 · 连接请求（Connection Note，≤300 字符）**

```
Hi [Client A name], I noticed you're in the kitchenware distribution space.
We manufacture chef knives and shears in Yangjiang, China — would love to
follow your work. No pitch, just connecting.
```

> 不要在连接请求里发链接/报价。先通过好友。

**Step 2 · 首次私信（通过后 1–2 天）**

```
Hi [Client A name], thanks for connecting! I saw your post about [他发的内容/他公司的品类].
Just curious — what's your main product category these days?
```

> 目的：**提问让对方开口**，不要自说自话。

**Step 3 · 价值提供（对方回复后）**

```
Thanks for sharing. We actually work with [类似类型] brands in [his country].
One thing that usually helps: I can send over a 1-page spec sheet on [his品类],
plus a factory video so you can see how we make them. Want me to send it?
```

> 给的是"资料"，不是"报价"。

**Step 4 · 邀约通话（对方表现兴趣后）**

```
Would a 15-minute call next week work? I can show you the production line on
video and answer any questions. Here's my calendar: [Calendly link].
```

### 2.2 LinkedIn 完整英文对话示例

```
You:    Hi [Client A], thanks for connecting! Saw you're with [his company]
        in kitchenware. What are your best-selling categories right now?

Client A: We mainly do chef knives and cutlery sets for the US market.

You:    Nice — that's exactly our strength. We make 8" chef knives from
        German 1.4116 steel, MOQ [示例: 500 pcs]. Can I send a spec sheet
        and a short factory video?

Client A: Sure, send it.

You:    Done (sent to this DM + here's the link [pdf]). Based in Yangjiang,
        we work with brands that care about edge quality. Want a 15-min
        video call this week to see the line?

Client A: Maybe next week.
```

## 三、Instagram 私信开发剧本（B2C / DTC 品牌方）

### 3.1 三步：评论互动 → 私信 → 产品介绍 → 报价

**Step 1 · 评论互动（先混脸熟）**
- 在目标品牌的帖子下留**有价值**的评论（不是 "Great!"）：
  "Love the edge geometry on this one. Are these VG-10?"

**Step 2 · 私信（评论互动 1–2 天后）**

```
Hey [Client B]! Really love what you're doing with your knife brand —
your product photography is on point. I'm Leo from Yangjiang, we manufacture
chef knives for a few DTC brands. Not pitching anything, just wanted to connect.
```

**Step 3 · 产品介绍（对方回复后）**

```
Thanks for the reply! If you ever need a reliable OEM partner for chef knives,
we can do private label with your logo, custom packaging, MOQ from
[示例: 300 pcs]. Here's our catalog: [link]. Want me to send samples?
```

### 3.2 Instagram 完整对话示例

```
You:    Hey [Client B]! Love your knife content — the Damascus posts look
        unreal. I'm Leo, we make knives in Yangjiang. Just connecting.

Client B: Oh thanks! How can I help?

You:    Actually, we're a manufacturer. If you ever think about launching
        your own line, we do private label — your logo, your packaging,
        MOQ [示例: 300]. Samples are cheap. Want a quote?

Client B: Send me your bestseller chef knife pricing.

You:    Done — check your email (I got it from your bio). Let me know
        what you think!
```

## 四、Facebook 私信开发剧本（B2B 群组 / 主页）

### 4.1 路径：主页互动 → Messenger → 开发

1. 先在目标公司主页/行业群组点赞、评论 2–3 次（建立存在感）。
2. 再发 Messenger：

```
Hi [Client C], I've been following your page and love the kitchenware
content you share. I'm Leo from KaiLionCrafts — we manufacture knives
in Yangjiang. Would you be open to a quick intro? No pressure either way.
```

3. 对方回复后，同 LinkedIn Step 3–4 推进价值提供与通话邀约。

> Facebook 群组开发：先在群里回答别人问题、输出价值 1–2 周，再私信群里活跃的买家。

## 五、私信频率与防封注意事项

| 平台 | 每日私信上限（经验值） | 防封要点 |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 新账号每周连接请求 ≤ 20–30 个；养号 1 个月后再加量 | 不要批量发相同文案；先互动再私信 |
| Instagram | 新号每日 DM ≤ 20–30 条 | 不要发外部链接太快；先聊几句再发链接 |
| Facebook | 新号每日消息 ≤ 15–20 条 | 不要加陌生人太快；先在群组活跃 |

**通用防封铁律**：
1. **文案不要批量复制**：每一条换称呼、换一两个词，避免被判 spam。
2. **先互动后私信**：点赞/评论/关注 1–2 次再发 DM。
3. **不要第一条就甩链接+报价**：至少聊 2–3 个回合再给资料。
4. **被已读不回就停**：同一人最多跟进 2 次，然后转邮件长线。
5. **账号养号期**：新号前 2 周只发内容、不发开发私信。
6. 更多平台防封见 `07_营销与社媒/执行与防封/` 目录。
