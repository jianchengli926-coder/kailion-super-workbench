# 实战剧本09：危机处理剧本（3 大场景）

> **核心原则**（来自 `03_客户开发与CRM/客户服务全流程SOP.md`）：
> 危机处理的黄金 24 小时——**先认人，再认事，最后认钱**。客户发火时先要的是"有人负责"，不是"责任划分"。
> 顺序：**响应（2h 内）→ 调查（24h 内）→ 方案（48h 内）→ 执行（7 天内）→ 预防（30 天内）**。
> ⚠️ 所有价格/MOQ/数量为示例数据，非公司实际定价。

---

## 场景 1：客户投诉质量问题（德国客户，50/1000 把刃口瑕疵）

### 场景背景

| 项 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 客户 | Client B（德国法兰克福进口商，剧本 07 场景二的老客户） |
| 订单 | 1,000 pcs KL-KN-SS-012 主厨刀，FOB Shenzhen，已到港 2 周 |
| 问题 | 客户入库 QC 发现 **50 把（5%）刃口有瑕疵**：刃口有微小崩口/磨偏 |
| 客户情绪 | 愤怒但克制，德国客户要书面 root cause + 解决方案 |
| 风险 | 若处理不当，德国客户会取消后续季度订单（年采购 €800K+） |

---

### Round 1 — 客户投诉邮件（周一早上 09:00 德国时间）

**客户邮件：**

> **Subject: URGENT — Quality issue on order KL-PI-2026-047 (1,000 pcs chef knife)**
>
> Leo,
>
> We have completed incoming QC on the 1,000 pcs KL-KN-SS-012.
>
> **50 pieces (5.0%) have blade edge defects**: small chips along the cutting edge, and on ~15 pieces the edge is ground off-center. Photos attached.
>
> This is unacceptable. Our agreement was <0.5% defect rate. We cannot ship these to our retail customers.
>
> We expect:
> 1. A written root cause analysis within 48 hours
> 2. A clear proposal for replacement or credit
> 3. Preventive measures so this does not repeat
>
> Please advise urgently.
>
> — Bauer

**策略分析：**
- 德国客户投诉邮件**结构化、带数字、带照片、带截止时间**——这是好事，不是坏事。说明他在按流程走，不是情绪化发火。
- 5% 不良率确实远超合同 0.5%，我方理亏，不能辩解。
- **48 小时 root cause 是硬要求**，必须做到。

---

### Round 2 — 我方第一响应（2 小时内，不超过当天）

**我方回件（德国时间 11:00，2 小时后）：**

> Dear Mr. Bauer,
>
> Thank you for the detailed QC report and photos. I am sorry this happened — this is not the standard we agreed on.
>
> I have already:
> 1. Pulled the production batch records for order KL-PI-2026-047
> 2. Asked our QC manager to inspect the remaining stock from the same batch
> 3. Contacted the grinding workshop to identify the root cause
>
> **Preliminary finding (preliminary, not final):** The edge chips are consistent with a grinding wheel that was not dressed on schedule — a process lapse on our side. I will confirm with the QC manager and send a full written root cause by tomorrow afternoon.
>
> **Provisional solution (we will formalize tomorrow):**
> - We will **replace all 50 defective pieces** at our cost (including freight to Hamburg)
> - We will ship the replacements together with your next order, or by air if you need them sooner
> - We will credit the inspection cost back to you
>
> I will call you tomorrow at 10:00 your time to walk through the root cause.
>
> Mit freundlichen Grüßen,
> Leo

**策略分析：**
- **2 小时内响应**：危机处理黄金时间。哪怕还没查清楚，先告诉客户"我在动"。
- **不辩解、不甩锅**：直接说"a process lapse on our side"。德国客户最恨供应商找借口。
- **给初步方案但不锁死**：说"provisional solution"，留明天正式方案的空间。
- **主动约电话**：书面之外加一通电话，显重视。
- ⚠️ 不要在没查清楚前说"we'll give you 100% refund"——那是过度承诺。

---

### Round 3 — 正式 root cause + 解决方案（24h 后）

**我方邮件：**

> Dear Mr. Bauer,
>
> As promised, here is the formal root cause and solution.
>
> **Root cause analysis:**
>
> | Step | Finding |
> |---|---|
> | Production batch | Heat treatment was normal (HRC 56.8–57.5 on random check) |
> | Grinding stage | The grinding wheel on Line 2 was dressed 400 pcs later than scheduled. A worn wheel causes micro-chipping on the edge. |
> | QC stage | The pre-shipment QC sample (20 pcs) was drawn from the first 100 pcs of the run — before the wheel wore. The defective 50 pcs came from pcs 600–900, which we did not sample. |
> | Contributing factor | Our QC sampling plan was AQL 2.5, which allows up to 2.5% defects. At 5% actual, we should have caught this. |
>
> This is entirely our process failure.
>
> **Solution (we propose):**
>
> 1. **Replace 50 defective pieces** free of charge, including DHL air freight to Hamburg (~$180 freight, our cost).
> 2. **Credit back 2% of the order value** (€110, example) as a goodwill gesture for your warehouse inspection cost.
> 3. **For your next order**:
>    - We upgrade QC sampling to **AQL 1.0** (tighter)
>    - We dress the grinding wheel every 300 pcs (was 700)
>    - We send you a **pre-shipment QC video covering the last 200 pcs of the run** (not just the first 100)
>
> We will also send a corrected QC manual extract showing the new sampling frequency.
>
> Please confirm if this is acceptable. We can have the 50 replacements ready in 7 days.
>
> — Leo

**策略分析：**
- **Root cause 写到工序级**：德国客户要的是"哪一道工序、为什么、怎么改"，不是"we'll be more careful"。
- **方案三层**：换货（解决眼前）+ 信用返还（补偿成本）+ 流程改进（防止再发）。
- **不裸退全款**：5% 不良 = 换 50 把 + 2% 信用，这是合理的 B2B 赔偿。全款退会让客户觉得订单本身有问题。
- 升级到 AQL 1.0 + 后段取样视频——这是可验证的预防措施。

---

### Round 4 — 客户确认（1 天后）

**客户邮件：**

> Dear Mr. Li,
>
> The root cause analysis is satisfactory. We accept the solution.
>
> Please ship the 50 replacements together with our next order (expected December), not by air — there is no rush.
>
> One request: in the next order, please also send 50 spare pieces free (so we have buffer for our own outgoing QC).
>
> — Bauer

**策略分析：**
- 客户接受方案，且不要空运（说明他不是要狮子大开口，是要流程正确）。
- 他额外要 50 把 free spares——这是合理的缓冲需求，答应。

---

### Round 5 — 我方确认 + 闭环

**我方邮件：**

> Dear Mr. Bauer,
>
> Agreed. For your December order:
> - 1,000 pcs KL-KN-SS-012 at the agreed price
> - **50 replacement pieces** (free)
> - **50 free spares** (your buffer request)
> - Total 1,100 pcs at the 1,000 pcs price
>
> AQL 1.0 sampling + late-run QC video will be in place.
>
> Thank you for your patience and for working through this with us. We value the relationship and will earn back your trust on the next batch.
>
> — Leo

### 质量投诉处理 SOP

| 步骤 | 时限 | 动作 | 禁忌 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. 第一响应 | 2 小时内 | 邮件确认收到 + 已在调查 + 电话/邮件约下一步 | 不要说 "we'll look into it" 就消失 |
| 2. 临时安抚 | 当天 | 给 provisional solution（不锁死） | 不要当场答应全款退/全部重发 |
| 3. Root cause | 48 小时 | 工序级原因 + 证据（批次记录/照片） | 不要说 "human error" 了事 |
| 4. 正式方案 | 72 小时 | 换货/退款/折扣组合 + 预防措施 | 不要只换货不改流程 |
| 5. 执行 | 7 天 | 换货发出 + 跟踪号 | 不要拖延 |
| 6. 预防验证 | 下一批 | 把新 QC 流程写进 PI 附件 | 不要口头承诺不落地 |

### 赔偿标准参考（示例，非公司政策）

| 不良率 | 推荐赔偿方案 |
|---|---|
| <1% | 免费补发等量备品，下一批随货 |
| 1%–3% | 补发不良数量 + 1%–2% 信用返还 |
| 3%–5% | 补发不良数量 + 3%–5% 信用 + 升级 QC |
| 5%–10% | 换货 + 5%–10% 折扣 + 我方承担往返运费 |
| >10% | 整批重做或退货 + 协商赔偿；考虑中信保/律师介入 |

## 场景 2：交期延误（美国客户，热处理设备故障延误 15 天）

### 场景背景

| 项 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 客户 | Client A（美国 Amazon 卖家，剧本 07 场景一） |
| 订单 | 1,000 pcs KL-OD-SS-012 折叠刀，原定 10 月 20 日发货 |
| 问题 | 工厂热处理炉故障，维修需 15 天，**交期延误至 11 月 4 日** |
| 客户痛点 | Amazon FBA 断货 = listing 排名掉，损失广告费 |
| 风险 | 美国客户决策快，延误可能直接换供应商 |

---

### Round 1 — 我方主动通知（发现故障当天，提前 15 天）

**我方邮件（北京周一 09:00，美西周日 18:00 定时发，客户周一早上看到）：**

> **Subject: Heads up — your order [PO number] may be delayed 15 days**
>
> Hi [Client Name],
>
> I want to give you a heads-up as early as I can. Our factory's heat treatment furnace had a mechanical failure over the weekend. The repair technician estimates 15 days to fix.
>
> **Current timeline:**
> - Original ship date: Oct 20
> - **New ship date: Nov 4** (15 days later)
> - This affects your 1,000 pcs KL-OD-SS-012 order
>
> I know this is bad timing — you're building up for Q4. I want to make this right:
>
> **Option A — We air-freight 200 pcs from our LA warehouse** on day 5 (so you have 200 pcs in FBA within 7 days to avoid stock-out). The remaining 800 pcs go by sea on Nov 4.
>
> **Option B — We ship all 1,000 pcs by sea on Nov 4, and give you a 5% discount on this order (~$100 credit) for the inconvenience.**
>
> **Option C — We split the difference**: 200 pcs from LA stock by air (our cost), remaining 800 by sea, and a 3% discount.
>
> My recommendation is **Option C** — it protects your FBA listing while sharing the pain.
>
> Please let me know by Wednesday which you prefer. I know this is not what you wanted to hear Monday morning, and I'm sorry.
>
> — Leo

**策略分析：**
- **提前 15 天通知，不是临期通知**：交期危机最大的错误是"到了发货日才说发不出"。提前预警本身就是专业。
- **3 个选项给客户选**：不是"对不起我们延误了"，而是"这是 3 个补救方案"。
- **主动提 LA 仓**：把我们的差异化卖点变成危机补救工具。
- **折扣是主动给**：不要等客户开口要赔偿。5% 折扣买不断货，比客户跑掉便宜。
- **结尾真诚道歉**：美国客户吃"我知道时机不好，对不起"这套，不端着。

---

### Round 2 — 客户回复（周一中午）

**客户邮件：**

> Leo,
>
> Not great timing — my FBA stock runs out on Oct 25. If I go stock-out for 15 days, my BSR ranking drops and I'll lose my PPC campaign position.
>
> Option C sounds best. 200 pcs from LA by air — how fast can they land at FBA?
>
> And the 3% discount — apply it to this order.
>
> — [Client Name]

**策略分析：**
- 客户选了 Option C——符合预期。
- 他说了真实痛点：FBA 断货 15 天 = BSR 掉排名。这是 Amazon 卖家最怕的事。
- 要给 LA 仓 200pcs 落地 FBA 的精确时间。

---

### Round 3 — 我方确认补救细节

**我方邮件：**

> Hi [Client Name],
>
> Option C confirmed.
>
> **LA warehouse dispatch (tomorrow):**
> - 200 pcs KL-OD-SS-012, polybag + FNSKU + suffocation warning (FBA prep)
> - Ship to your FBA warehouse [FBA code]
> - Transit: 3–5 days to FBA inbound
> - **Expected FBA check-in: Oct 20–22** — before your stock-out on Oct 25
>
> **Sea shipment (Nov 4):**
> - Remaining 800 pcs
> - ETA LA port: Nov 18
> - FBA check-in: Nov 22
>
> **Discount:** 3% = $46.80 credited on this order (new total $1,568.20 instead of $1,615).
>
> I'll send you the LA tracking tomorrow, and weekly updates on the furnace repair.
>
> — Leo

**策略分析：**
- **给精确到天的时间线**：美国客户要的是可操作的计划，不是"soon"。
- **算好日期**：LA 200pcs 在 Oct 20–22 到 FBA，赶在 Oct 25 断货前——这是关键。
- 每周同步维修进度，不让客户瞎猜。

---

### Round 4 — 客户接受（当天）

**客户邮件：**

> OK. LA tracking tomorrow.
>
> For future orders — can we build in a 2-week buffer on your lead time? I'd rather place the order 2 weeks earlier than risk another stock-out.
>
> — [Client Name]

**策略分析：**
- 客户没跑，反而在谈未来订单的缓冲期——这是危机转化为信任的机会。
- 答应缓冲期，并把它写进未来 PO 条款。

---

### 交期延误应对策略

| 原则 | 做法 |
|---|---|
| 提前预警 | 发现风险立刻通知，不要等到期当天 |
| 给选项 | 至少 2–3 个补救方案（空运/分批/折扣） |
| 主动补偿 | 5% 以内折扣主动给，不要等客户索赔 |
| 用现有资源 | LA 仓现货/分批发货是我们的差异化武器 |
| 频率透明 | 每周同步维修进度，不要让客户催 |
| 转化信任 | 把"缓冲期"写进未来 PO，危机变流程改进 |

### 何时可能流失客户
- 临期才通知（提前 <3 天）
- 只道歉不给方案
- 客户两次催才回复
- 同一供应商第二次延误
- 没有补偿态度

## 场景 3：价格争议（印度客户，以竞品为由要求降价 20%）

### 场景背景

| 项 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 客户 | Client D（印度孟买进口商，剧本 07 场景四） |
| 订单 | 已下首单 5,000 pcs 厨房剪，大货生产中 |
| 问题 | 客户突然说"另一中国供应商报 $0.85，你 $1.05，要求降 20%" |
| 风险 | 印度客户可能以此为由拒付尾款/要求重谈 |

---

### Round 1 — 客户邮件（生产中途）

**客户邮件：**

> Leo,
>
> One more thing before production finishes.
>
> Another Chinese supplier quoted me **$0.85/pc** for 5,000 pcs stainless kitchen shears — same appearance. Yours is $1.05.
>
> That's 20% difference. I need you to match $0.85, otherwise I may have to split the order and buy from them.
>
> This is my last order discussion — please reply fast.
>
> — [Client Name]

**策略分析：**
- **生产中途压价**——这是印度客户的经典套路。货已经在做了，想趁机再砍一刀。
- 判断：$0.85 的货大概率是 2Cr13 + 塑料手柄，不是我们的 420J2 + PP+TPR。
- **不能慌、不能裸降**。一旦生产中途降价，后续每一单都会被这么拿捏。

---

### Round 2 — 我方不拒绝，先把差异摆清楚（24h 内）

**我方邮件：**

> Hi [Client Name],
>
> I understand — price matters. Let me make sure we're comparing the same product.
>
> At $0.85/pc FOB for stainless shears, the typical spec is:
> - Steel: 2Cr13 (lower grade, rusts faster)
> - Handle: basic PP plastic (no TPR soft grip)
> - Pivot: solid rivet (not adjustable)
> - No serration
>
> Our $1.05/pc (KL-SC-SS-005) is:
> - Steel: 420J2 stainless (better corrosion)
> - Handle: PP + TPR soft grip
> - Adjustable cross-screw pivot
> - Micro-serration on one blade
>
> If you genuinely want the 2Cr13 / basic version, I can quote that too — it would be around **$0.88/pc at 5,000 pcs**. But that's a different product, and your retail customers will feel the difference in 6 months.
>
> On this order: production is already 60% complete on the $1.05 spec. We cannot change steel mid-production. But for your **next order**, I can offer:
>
> - 5,000 pcs 420J2 (current spec): **$1.00/pc** (down from $1.05)
> - Or 5,000 pcs 2Cr13 (budget spec): **$0.88/pc**
>
> You choose the product tier for next time. This order ships as confirmed.
>
> — Leo

**策略分析：**
- **不拒绝，给两个 tier**：把客户的"降价 20%"拆成"你要哪个档次"。
- **明确说本单不能改**：生产已 60%，改钢是质量事故。
- **下单价让 $0.05**（从 $1.05 → $1.00），但换客户不撤单。这是"有条件让步"。
- 给 2Cr13 选项 $0.88——让客户自己选是不是真要便宜货。

---

### Round 3 — 客户继续压（可能）

**客户可能回复：**

> Leo,
>
> $1.00 is still not $0.85. The other supplier will give me 30 days credit too.
>
> If you can't match, I'll pay this order but buy next time from them.
>
> — [Client Name]

**策略分析：**
- 客户开始拿账期+竞品双重压。这是印度谈判的最后一搏。
- 我方底线：本单 $1.05 不动；下单价 $1.00 是地板。再低就不做了。

---

### Round 4 — 我方最终立场（不卑不亢）

**我方邮件：**

> Hi [Client Name],
>
> I respect that you're running a business on tight margins.
>
> Here's my final position:
>
> - **This order**: ships as confirmed at $1.05/pc. Production continues.
> - **Next order**: $1.00/pc on the 420J2 spec (5,000 pcs), 30% T/T deposit + 70% before shipment.
> - **30-day credit**: we don't offer OA to any customer — it's company policy, not personal. If that's a dealbreaker, I understand.
>
> If you want to test the other supplier first, that's fine. When you're ready, we're here.
>
> Either way, this order will ship on time.
>
> — Leo

**策略分析：**
- **明确划线**：本单不动、下单价地板 $1.00、不做 OA。
- **给客户选择权**："you can go test the other supplier"——不卑微。
- 印度客户很多时候是试探，你一让他就继续压；你坚定了，他反而留下。
- **不威胁、不生气**：冷静陈述事实。

---

### 价格争议应对策略

| 原则 | 做法 |
|---|---|
| 不裸降 | 每次降价绑条件（量/付款/未来订单） |
| 拆 tier | 把"降价"变成"换产品档次" |
| 生产中不重谈 | 已排产的订单按 PO 走 |
| OA 不松口 | 新客户不做赊销，中信保是例外 |
| 给台阶 | "you can test the other supplier"——不卑微 |
| 何时放弃 | 客户要求低于成本价 + 要求 OA + 生产中反悔——放弃，不做这单 |

### 何时应该放弃客户

- 客户要求价格低于我方成本（不是利润薄，是亏）
- 要求 100% OA 或 D/A（无中信保）
- 生产中途反复改价
- 用投诉/拒付尾款威胁
- 同时在跟 3 家以上比价且每次都拿最低价压

> **判断标准**：这单如果做了亏钱 + 收款风险高，不做比做好。印度市场大，下一个客户会来。

## 三场景危机处理通用心法

1. **2 小时内必须响应**：哪怕只说"received, investigating"
2. **不当场认所有责任**：先 provisional，再正式 root cause
3. **给选项而不是给道歉**：客户要的是解决方案
4. **赔偿要可验证**：不口头承诺，写进 PI/邮件
5. **危机是信任加分项**：处理得好，客户比没出事时更忠诚
6. **不卑不亢**：理亏时大方赔，被讹时坚定拒
