# 询盘回复模板

> **使用说明**：客户主动发来询盘后24小时内回复。
> 回复结构：感谢 → 确认需求 → 给初步信息 → CTA。

---

## 一、标准询盘回复模板

**中文说明**：客户通过独立站/邮件发来询盘后的标准回复。先感谢、确认需求、问清楚关键信息，再约正式报价。

**Subject:** Re: [original subject] — KaiLionCrafts

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Thank you for your inquiry and for your interest in KaiLionCrafts!
>
> I'd be happy to help. To give you an accurate quote, could you confirm a few details:
>
> 1. **Which product(s)** are you interested in? (Kitchen knives / Scissors / Outdoor knives / Kitchen tools)
> 2. **What quantity** are you looking for? (per model / total order)
> 3. **Do you need custom logo / packaging?** (OEM/ODM)
> 4. **Which certifications** are required for your market? (FDA / LFGB / CE / others)
> 5. **Target price range**, if you have one?
>
> Once I have these details, I'll send you a formal quotation within 48 hours.
>
> In the meantime, here's our product catalog for your reference: [link]
>
> Best regards,
> Leo Li
> KaiLionCrafts
> WhatsApp: +86 131-3800-6564
> Web: kailioncrafts.com

## 二、客户问"Send me your price list"

**中文说明**：客户只说"发价格表"，没有具体需求。先给区间，再引导问具体产品。

**Subject:** Price list & catalog — KaiLionCrafts

> Hi [First Name],

## 三、客户问"Can you send samples?"

**中文说明**：客户索样时的标准回复，说明样品政策。

**Subject:** Samples — arrangement & policy

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Yes, we'd be happy to send samples!
>
> Our sample policy:
> - **Sample fee**: $[X.XX] per piece (varies by model)
> - **DHL shipping**: [X-XX] days, cost depends on your address
> - **Sample fee is deductible** from your first bulk order
> - **Custom logo samples**: one-time mold/engraving fee of $50
>
> Could you provide:
> 1. Which model(s) you'd like to test?
> 2. Your shipping address (with zip code)?
> 3. Your DHL/FedEx account number (if you prefer to collect)?
>
> I'll send the sample invoice and arrange shipment within 24 hours.
>
> Best,
> Leo

## 四、报价单结构说明

报价单（Quotation）应包含以下要素，以PDF格式发送：

```
===============================================
KaiLionCrafts
Yangjiang Kaili International Trading Co., Ltd.
QUOTATION
===============================================

Quotation No.: KLC-Q-2026-001
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Valid until: [YYYY-MM-DD + 30 days]

To: [Client Company Name]
Attn: [Contact Person]
Address: [Client Address]

-----------------------------------------------
ITEM | DESCRIPTION | QTY | UNIT PRICE (FOB) | TOTAL
-----------------------------------------------
1 | Model K001 8" Chef Knife | 500pcs | $X.XX | $X,XXX.XX
   | 5Cr15MoV, HRC56-58, Pakkawood handle
2 | Model S003 Professional Scissors | 500pcs | $X.XX | $X,XXX.XX
   | 420J2 stainless, soft-grip handle
...

TOTAL FOB Shenzhen: $X,XXX.XX
-----------------------------------------------

Terms:
- Price: FOB Shenzhen, China
- Payment: 30% T/T deposit, 70% against B/L copy
- Lead time: 30-35 days after deposit
- MOQ: as stated above
- Packaging: color box / gift box (custom available)
- Warranty: 1-year quality guarantee

Notes:
- Prices valid for 30 days
- Custom logo/packaging available on request
- SGS LFGB / FDA / CE / RoHS certifications available

===============================================
Leo Li | KaiLionCrafts
WhatsApp: +86 131-3800-6564
Web: kailioncrafts.com
```

## 五、样品政策说明（附在报价单后）

```
- Free samples for repeat/verified customers
```
