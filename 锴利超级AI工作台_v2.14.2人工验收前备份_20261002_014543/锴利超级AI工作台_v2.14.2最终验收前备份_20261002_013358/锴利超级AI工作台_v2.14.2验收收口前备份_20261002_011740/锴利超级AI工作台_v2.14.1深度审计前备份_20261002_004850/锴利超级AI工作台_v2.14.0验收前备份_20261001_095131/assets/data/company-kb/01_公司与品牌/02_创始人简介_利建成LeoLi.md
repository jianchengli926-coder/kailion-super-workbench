## 三、创业故事（核心叙事）

利建成成长于阳江五金刀剪产业环境之中，家族成员长期深耕本地刀剪及五金制造领域，使其从小对阳江刀剪产业链、产品工艺、材料应用和供应链协作形成直接认知。

在广州多家外贸企业工作期间，他逐渐看到传统阳江制造与海外市场之间的明显断层：本地工厂有制造能力，却缺少系统化的海外品牌表达；海外买家有采购需求，却常被信息不透明、中间环节复杂、营销素材不足和小批量定制难等问题困扰。基于这一判断，他于 2025 年回到阳东创业，创办锴利国际，希望用 AI、独立站、SEO、短视频内容和本地供应链协同，为阳江制造建立新的数字化出海路径。

他选择的不是传统"平台铺货 + 低价竞争"模式，而是以 AI 工具和数字化系统降低创业成本，以独立站和内容营销提升品牌信任，以本地供应链协同提高交付稳定性。他代表了一类新的阳东青年创业方向：既扎根本地制造产业，又具备全球市场视野；既理解传统工厂生产逻辑，又掌握 AI、网站、SEO、短视频等新型数字工具。

## 六、English Founder Profile

Leo Li, founder and CEO of Yangjiang Kaili International Trading Co., Ltd. and brand owner of KaiLionCrafts, is a young entrepreneur born and raised in Yangdong District, Yangjiang. Growing up in the local hardware and cutlery manufacturing environment, he developed a strong understanding of Yangjiang's supply chain, product craftsmanship, and manufacturing ecosystem.

Before founding the company in 2025, Leo gained hands-on experience in foreign trade, digital marketing, content creation, and supply chain coordination through several foreign trade roles in Guangzhou. He later returned to Yangdong to build KaiLionCrafts as a digital export brand combining AI tools, independent website operation, Google SEO, visual content production, and local supply chain integration.

Leo's core strength lies in his full-stack capability: AI-assisted workflow design, website building, SEO planning, product data organization, photography and video production, overseas customer development, and OEM/ODM project coordination. His goal is not only to operate a foreign trade company, but also to help Yangjiang manufacturing become more visible, trusted, and competitive in the global market.
