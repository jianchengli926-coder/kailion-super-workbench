## 二、出口五阶段流程（Export Workflow）

```
① Order Confirmation（确认订单）
   ↓  PI issued, deposit received, spec sheet locked
② Production & In-Line QC（生产 + 制程质检）
   ↓  Random checks at forging, heat treat, grinding, polishing stages
③ Packaging & Labelling（包装 & 贴标）
   ↓  Custom box, barcode, shipping marks per your requirement
④ Pre-Shipment Inspection（出货前检验）
   ↓  100% visual check + AQL sampling; photos sent before loading
⑤ Export & Logistics（出口 & 物流）
   ↓  Booked with vetted forwarders; tracking shared from departure to arrival
```

## 四、出口合规卖点

> **Certifications & Zero Regulatory Risk**

- **130+ 国家出口经验**：熟悉欧美/东南亚/中东/拉美各国刀具进口要求。
- **刀具出口 100% 合法**：PSB 公安局备案，不存在"被扣关"风险。
- **产品认证齐全**：FDA（美国食品接触）、LFGB（德国）、CE（欧盟）、BSCI（社会责任）、ISO9001（质量体系）。
- 户外刀另注：不同国家对刀刃长度/锁定机制有不同法律限制，下单前我们会主动提醒（见 `04_国家地区开发指南/户外刀法规专项/`）。

## 五、物流方案（Service Scope）

| 方式 | 适合 | 时效 |
|------|------|------|
| 海运整柜 FCL | 大批量补货（>500kg） | 25-35 天 |

## 六、FAQ（页面底部可展开）

**Q: What certifications do I need to import knives into my country?**
A: For the US: FDA food-contact compliance (already included). For the EU: CE + LFGB. For the UK: UKCA. We provide all required docs with every shipment — just tell us your destination.

**Q: Can you handle DDP (delivered duty paid) shipping?**
A: Yes. We offer DDP to major US and EU destinations. You get one all-in price, no surprise customs fees.

**Q: What if my goods are held by customs?**
A: We've shipped to 130+ countries and our documentation is pre-checked. Holds are extremely rare. If anything comes up, we handle it directly — you don't pay storage fees caused by our paperwork errors.

**Q: Do you provide PSB / export registration for knives?**
A: Yes. All knife exports are PSB-registered (Chinese Public Security Bureau), ensuring legal departure from China and smooth entry for your customs broker.
