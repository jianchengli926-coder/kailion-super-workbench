# Amazon Seller Operations Guide / Amazon 卖家运营指南

> 说明（中文）：本指南为 KaiLionCrafts 五金刀剪品类在 Amazon 美国站为主、欧洲站为辅的运营手册。模板字段用英文，关键决策与合规红线用中文标注。

---

## 1. Account Registration / 账号注册

### 1.1 Selling Plans / 销售计划

| Plan | Cost | Best For |
|------|------|----------|
| **Individual** | $0.99 per item sold, no monthly fee | <40 units/month, testing SKUs |
| **Professional** | **$39.99/month** (US), €39/month (EU), £25/month (UK) | Steady sales, advertising, bulk upload, Brand Registry |

- **KaiLionCrafts recommendation**: Start with **Professional** once monthly volume >40 units; required for Sponsored Ads, A+ Content, Brand Registry.
- Source: https://sell.amazon.com/pricing (retrieved 2026-09-27)

### 1.2 Global Selling / 全球开店

- Use a **single Professional account** to unlock multiple marketplaces (US, CA, MX, UK, DE, FR, IT, ES, JP).
- Onboard via **Amazon Global Selling** (https://sell.amazon.com/global-selling) for cross-border tax/VAT setup.
- EU expansion requires **VAT registration** in UK + DE + FR (IOSS for <€150 shipments).

### 1.3 KYC Verification / KYC 审核

- Required for **EU/UK/JP** accounts and for any account reaching certain volume thresholds.
- Documents: business license, bank statement (≤180 days), passport/ID of beneficial owner, proof of address.
- **中文提示**：KYC 被拒常见原因 = 地址证明与营业执照不一致、银行账单无银行公章、受益人股权结构超过 25% 未披露。提前准备彩色扫描件，不要 PS。

## 2. Listing Optimization / Listing 优化

### 2.1 Title Template (EN)

```
[Brand] + [Product Type] + [Material/Steel Grade] + [Key Feature 1] + [Key Feature 2] + [Size/Usage]
Example: KaiLionCrafts 8-Inch Chef Knife, German 1.4116 Stainless Steel, Full Tang, Razor Sharp Edge, Kitchen Cooking Knife
```

- Length: ≤200 chars (Amazon US soft limit; mobile truncation at ~60 chars).
- **Do NOT** put promotional words (Best, #1, Free Shipping, Sale).
- Lead with the highest-search-volume keyword (use Helium 10 / Jungle Scout).

### 2.2 Bullet Points (5 bullets, ~200 chars each)

1. **BENEFIT-led opening** (not feature): "Why you'll love this knife..."
2. Material & steel (e.g., X50CrMoV15 / 1.4116 / Damascus).
3. Ergonomics & balance (handle material, tang type).
4. Use cases (kitchen / camping / BBQ — **never "self-defense" or "combat"**).
5. Care & warranty ("Hand wash recommended; lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects").

### 2.3 A+ Content / EBC

- Requires **Brand Registry** (pending trademark, ~6-12 months USPTO).
- Modules: Brand Story, Comparison Chart, Lifestyle Images, Feature Highlights.
- For knives: emphasize **craftsmanship, heat-treating process, QC inspection** — matches 阳江 factory story.

### 2.4 Keyword Strategy

- Backend search terms: ≤250 bytes, no commas, no repeat words already in title.
- Prohibited words in any knife listing: `switchblade`, `automatic`, `spring-assisted` (if US listing), `tactical`, `combat`, `self-defense`, `zombie`, `Balisong`, `butterfly`, `OTF`.

### 2.5 Image Standards

- Main image: pure white background (RGB 255,255,255), product fills ≥85% frame, no text/watermark/logo.
- Minimum 1000 px on longest side (zoom-enabled).
- Allowed gallery images: lifestyle, size chart, infographic, packaging, hand-feel.
- **刀具红线**: 图片不得展示刀刃刺入人体/动物、不得展示攻击姿态、不得佩戴迷彩/战术背景。

## 3. FBA vs FBM / 配送方式

| Dimension | FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) | FBM (Merchant Fulfilled) |
|-----------|----------------------------|--------------------------|
| Prime badge | ✅ Automatic | ❌ Must qualify for Seller Fulfilled Prime |
| Storage fee | $0.87–$2.40/cu ft/month (standard); peak Q4 higher | Your own warehouse cost |
| Pick & pack fee | $3.0–$4.5/unit (standard size) | Your labor + shipping label |
| Customer service | Amazon handles returns/CS | You handle all |
| Best for | Fast-moving SKUs, <12-month turnover | Oversize, slow-moving, custom |

- **KaiLionCrafts**: FBA for top 20 SKUs (chef knives, parers, shears); FBM for bulk/case packs and oversized cleavers.
- **刀具注意**: FBA has hazmat review; knives are **not** hazmat but require age-restricted flag in UK/EU.

## 4. Advertising System / 广告体系

### 4.1 Three Ad Types

| Type | Full Name | Placement | Best For |
|------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **SP** | Sponsored Products | Search result pages + detail pages | Single-ASIN ranking, keyword defense (75-80% of spend) |
| **SB** | Sponsored Brands | Top-of-search banner + brand logo + 3 products | Brand awareness, requires Brand Registry |
| **SD** | Sponsored Display | Product pages + off-Amazon retargeting | Remarketing to viewers, competitor ASIN targeting |

- Source: https://ecombrainly.com/amazon-ad-types/ (retrieved 2026-09-27)

### 4.2 ACoS Targets

- **New product launch (0-60 days)**: Target ACoS 40-60% — rank building, accept loss.
- **Mature product (>60 days, BSR top 100 in subcategory)**: Target ACoS ≤25-30%.
- **TACoS** (Total Ad Sales / Total Sales): Healthy range 8-15% for kitchen tools.

### 4.3 Bidding Strategies

- **Dynamic bids – down only**: safe default for auto-campaigns.
- **Dynamic bids – up and down**: use for top-of-search placement during launches.
- **Fixed bids**: manual, when you have conversion data and want control.
- Placement multipliers: Top of Search +30-50% during launch; Product Pages -20% if wasteful.
- Source: https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GCU2BUWJH2W3A8Z7 (retrieved 2026-09-27)

## 5. Review Management / Review 管理

### 5.1 Vine Program

- Enroll up to 30 ASINs, 30 units each, $200/parent ASIN enrollment fee (2025 rate).
- Vine reviewers are unbiased; expect honest critical reviews. Best for new launches.

### 5.2 Compliant Solicitation

- Use **"Request a Review" button** (Seller Central) within 4-30 days of delivery.
- Insert product inserts: thank-you card with neutral customer-service contact. **Do NOT** offer incentives for positive reviews, do NOT ask for 5-star explicitly.

### 5.3 Negative Review Handling

- Within 24h: respond publicly, polite, offer replacement/refund.
- If review violates guidelines (off-topic, price complaint, contains PII): Report to Amazon.
- **中文提示**：差评不要刷评反击（risk of suspension）；通过售后私信解决，让买家自愿修改。

## 6. Account Health / 账号安全

### 6.1 Key Metrics

| Metric | Target | Threshold for Warning |
|--------|--------|----------------------|
| Order Defect Rate (ODR) | <1% | >1% |
| Late Dispatch Rate | <4% | >4% |
| Pre-ship Cancellation Rate | <0.5% | >0.5% |
| Valid Tracking Rate | >95% | <95% |

### 6.2 IP & Infringement Risks

- Do not copy other sellers' images/listing text.
- Trademark: register KaiLionCrafts with USPTO before Brand Registry.
- Patent: avoid patented mechanisms (e.g., certain assisted-opening designs).
- **刀具特别风险**: Amazon automatically takedowns listings flagged as "weapons"; appeal with photos + manufacturer declaration that product is a kitchen/utility tool, not a weapon.

## 7. ⚠️ Knife Category Policy / 刀具类目政策（重点）

### 7.1 Amazon US — Prohibited (cannot list)

Source: Amazon Explosives, Weapons and Related Items Policy (G200164950), retrieved 2026-09-27:

- **Switchblades / automatic knives** (button- or gravity-opened)
- **Gravity knives**
- **Stilettos**
- **Balisong / butterfly knives** (including training versions in some categories)
- **Cane swords / shobi-zue / swordsticks** (concealed swords)
- **Knives disguised as harmless objects** (pens, belt buckles, lipsticks, combs)
- **Throwing knives, throwing stars, nunchucks**
- Items illegal in **any US state** (Amazon requires legality in all 50 states, except narrow CA carve-outs)

### 7.2 Amazon US — Allowed

- **Kitchen / chef / paring / bread / boning knives** ✅
- **Manual folding pocket knives** (no spring assist) — subject to state law
- **Hunting / camping / fixed-blade utility knives** marketed as tools
- **Multi-tools, utility knives, razor blades in cartridges**
- **Cutlery sets**

### 7.3 Amazon Ads Creative Policy (§5.8 Knives)

Source: https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/ad-policy/en (retrieved 2026-09-27):

- Prohibited in **ads** (even if product listing is allowed): knives marketed for combat/military/self-defense/violence — zombie knives, combat knives, throwing knives, daggers, boot knives, **spring-assisted knives**, hand-to-hand/fighting knives, gravity knives, bayonets, swords, ninja stars.
- **Implication**: Even if a spring-assisted knife can be listed, you **cannot run SP/SB ads** for it.

### 7.4 Amazon UK/EU — Stricter

Source: Amazon UK weapons policy (G201744080) + UK forum guidance, retrieved 2026-09-27:

- UK bans: machetes, kukuris, swords, daggers, zombie knives, any bladed article designed for injury.
- **Allowed in UK**: kitchen/chef knives; folding pocket knives with blade ≤3 inches (7.62 cm).
- **Age verification (OAV)**: Amazon blocks sale to under-18; seller must enable the "knife flag" on eligible listings.
- Germany: Springmesser (switchblades) banned under WaffG regardless of blade length.

### 7.5 State Law Notes (for US listings)

- **California**: Switchblade ≥2" blade = misdemeanor (Cal. Pen. Code §21510); 9th Circuit upheld ban Jan 2026. Own at home OK, but selling/shipping into CA restricted.
- **New York**: Gravity knife criminal repeal (2019), but NYC still bans public carry of blades >4".
- **Implication**: Even if Amazon policy allows a product, you must geo-block shipping to states where it's illegal.

### 7.6 Compliance Checklist for KaiLionCrafts Listings

- [ ] Product marketed as **kitchen/utility/camping tool**, never "tactical/self-defense/combat".
- [ ] No automatic/switchblade/gravity/OTF/balisong SKUs.
- [ ] Title/bullets/images free of weapon imagery.
- [ ] Age-restricted flag enabled on UK/EU listings.
- [ ] Shipping settings exclude states/countries where product is illegal.
- [ ] Packaging: boxed, blade guard included, "Keep out of reach of children" label.
