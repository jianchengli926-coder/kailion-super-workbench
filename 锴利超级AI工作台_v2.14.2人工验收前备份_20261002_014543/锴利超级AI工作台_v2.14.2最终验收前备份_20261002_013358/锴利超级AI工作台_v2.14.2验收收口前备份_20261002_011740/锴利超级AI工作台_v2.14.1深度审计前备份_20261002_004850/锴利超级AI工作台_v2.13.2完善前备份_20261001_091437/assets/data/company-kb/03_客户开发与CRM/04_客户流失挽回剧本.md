# 实战剧本04：客户流失挽回剧本

> **客户画像**：Marcus Johnson，Procurement Manager at "Culinary Pro Supply"（美国达拉斯厨房用品批发商，B+类客户）。
> **历史合作**：2026年3月首单 500pcs KL-KN-SS-012 黑檀柄主厨刀 @ $7.80 FOB，$3,900。5月复购300pcs。之后连续4个月无订单，8、9月发过2封跟进邮件未回。
> **流失信号识别**：B类客户60天无沟通（橙色信号）+ 8月曾在邮件中说"your price went up after the reorder"（红色信号——价格原因）。
> **链路**：识别预警 → Day1友好破冰 → 客户回复原因（价格高）→ 我方诊断 → 给方案（阶梯价/降配置）→ 客户试探 → 最终方案 → 重新下单。

## Round 4 — 我方诊断 + 给方案（价格原因专用话术）

**我方回件（9月20日，24h内响应）：**

> Hi Marcus,
>
> I really appreciate your honesty — that's exactly what I needed to hear.
>
> You're right about the price jump, and I want to own it. When you reordered at 300 pcs, the unit price moved to the 200-499 tier ($8.20). That's our standard volume pricing, but looking back, I should have flagged it proactively before you placed the order instead of letting you see it on the invoice. That's on me.
>
> Here's what I want to do to win the business back:
>
> **1. Re-align the price.** For Culinary Pro Supply, I'll honor the **500-pcs tier price of $7.80 on any order of 300 pcs or more**, for the next 12 months. No more surprises on reorder pricing.
>
> **2. Even better, if you can plan ahead:** If you commit to a quarterly order of 500+ pcs, I'll lock in **$7.20/pc** — that's below the $6.90 you're getting from Zhejiang when you factor in our pre-shipment QC video and the 10-year blade guarantee.
>
> **3. I'll also send 5 free sample units of our new KL-KN-SS-016 (forged German 1.4116 steel) so you can test whether it's worth adding to your catalog.**
>
> No obligation — just want you to know the door's open, and this time I'll price it transparently.
>
> Best,
> Leo

## Round 6 — 我方最终方案（降低门槛，小单试复购）

**我方回件：**

> Hi Marcus,
>
> Great questions:
>
> 1. **No contract needed** — your commitment is just placing a 500-pcs order each quarter. I'll put the $7.20 price in writing on the PI, valid for 12 months.
>
> 2. **Quality difference**: Our KL-KN-SS-012 runs HRC 56-58 (SGS tested), vs. the industry-typical HRC 52-55 from Zhejiang trading houses. That translates to roughly 2-3× longer edge retention. I'll attach:
>    - SGS LFGB + HRC lab report
>    - Our pre-shipment QC video (you approve before we ship)
>    - 10-year blade guarantee (free replacement if it dulls prematurely)
>
> 3. **200-pcs trial order**: Absolutely. Here's the deal:
>    - 200 pcs at **$7.80** (the old price you know)
>    - I'll throw in **4 free spare knives** (2%)
>    - If you're happy, your next order jumps to the quarterly framework at $7.20
>    - Lead time: 25 days (we'll prioritize you as a returning customer)
>
> Does that work? I'll have the PI ready tonight.
>
> Best,
> Leo

**挽回结果：** 客户从流失边缘 → 200pcs试复购($1,560) → 季度框架500pcs($3,600) + 200pcs剪刀 → 年采购稳定在$15K+。挽回成本仅为1封破冰邮件+4把备品，远低于开发新客户成本。

> **挽回纪律**（来自 `03_客户开发与CRM/17_客户流失预警与挽回.md` 第五节）：
> 1. 红色信号48小时内必须主动联系
> 2. 每次Lost客户必填流失原因标签
> 3. Win-back序列每月跑一批沉默客户
> 4. 挽回成功后重新走需求确认，不假设需求不变
> 5. 连续3轮Win-back无回应 → 降级D类，年度群发
