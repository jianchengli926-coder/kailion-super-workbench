# 跟进邮件序列（7封）

> **使用说明**：首封开发信发出后，按以下节奏跟进。
> 每封换一个角度，不要重复"just following up"。
> Breakup邮件通常回复率最高。

---

## 第1封：Day 0 — 首次开发信

> 见《01_首次开发信模板_多场景.md》对应场景模板。

---

## 第2封：Day 3 — 简单Bump

**中文说明**：怕邮件被淹没，简短提醒一下。不提任何新信息，就是轻推一下。

**Subject:** Following up: [original subject]

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Just floating this back to the top of your inbox in case it got buried.
>
> Would a quick 15-minute call this week work? Or should I send our catalog first?
>
> Best,
> Leo

---

## 第3封：Day 7 — 给物料降门槛

**中文说明**：附参数表/报价区间，给客户一个"不用回复也能看"的价值。同时附同行业案例。

**Subject:** Catalog & pricing for [product category]

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I know you're busy, so let me make this easy:
>
> Attached you'll find:
> 1. Our product catalog (4 categories: kitchen knives, scissors, outdoor knives, kitchen tools)
> 2. FOB price range by category
> 3. A quick case study: we recently helped a [similar company type] reduce their cutlery cost by 12% while maintaining LFGB certification
>
> No pressure to reply — just let me know if anything catches your eye and I'll send details.
>
> Best,
> Leo

---

## 第4封：Day 14 — 建立适配感

**中文说明**：讲行业应用场景和案例图片，让客户想象自己用我们的产品。

**Subject:** How other [client type] use our knives

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Thought you might find this useful: our kitchen knives are currently used by:
> - **[Type A, e.g. E-commerce brands]** for private-label FBA fulfillment
> - **[Type B, e.g. Boutique kitchen stores]** for premium retail
> - **[Type C, e.g. Restaurant supply distributors]** for commercial use
>
> The common feedback: consistent edge retention, reliable delivery, and OEM flexibility.
>
> Would it help if I sent photos of products similar to what you currently carry?
>
> Best,
> Leo

## 第5封：Day 21 — 降低判断成本

**中文说明**：给参数对比表，把我们和市面上常见产品的差异列清楚，减少客户决策成本。

**Subject:** Spec sheet: our 8" chef knife vs. typical market options

> Hi [First Name],
>
> To make your evaluation easier, here's a quick spec comparison:
>
> | Spec | KaiLionCrafts | Typical market |
> |---|---|---|
> | Steel | 5Cr15MoV (German 1.4116 option) | 3Cr13 |
> | Hardness | HRC 56-58 | HRC 52-55 |
> | Certification | SGS LFGB + FDA | Often none |
> | MOQ | 50pcs trial | 500pcs |
>
> Would you like a sample to verify? Sample fee is deductible from your first order.
>
> Best,
> Leo

## 第6封：Day 30 — 推进商务讨论

**中文说明**：给出明确报价区间和样品/付款安排，推进到实际商务讨论。

**Subject:** Pricing & sample arrangement for [product]

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I wanted to summarize where we are:
>
> - **Unit price** (FOB Shenzhen): $[X.XX] for [MOQ]pcs
> - **Sample**: $[XX.XX] (deductible from bulk order)
> - **Lead time**: 30-35 days after deposit
> - **Payment**: 30% T/T deposit, 70% against B/L copy
> - **Certifications**: LFGB, FDA, CE, RoHS, FSC available
>
> Shall I arrange the sample shipment? Or do you need more info first?
>
> Best,
> Leo

## 第7封：Day 30+ — Breakup（优雅收尾）

**中文说明**：这封通常回复率最高。告诉客户"这是我最后一封跟进"，把决定权交回给客户。很多人这时候才回复。

**Subject:** Should I close your file?

> Hi [First Name],
>
> This will be my last email for now. I completely understand if cutlery sourcing isn't a priority right now.
>
> If things change in the future — whether you need a new supplier, samples, or just a price comparison — feel free to reach out anytime. I'm happy to help.
>
> Wishing you and [Client Company] all the best.
>
> Warm regards,
> Leo Li
> KaiLionCrafts
> WhatsApp: +86 131-3800-6564

## 已读不回专用：ABC选项追问

**中文说明**：客户已读不回时，用ABC选项让客户用最少的字回复。

**Subject:** Quick question on next steps

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Seems we get stuck somewhere. To help me move forward, could you let me know:
>
> **A.** Price or payment term issue?
> **B.** Shipping/freight concern?
> **C.** Timing isn't right?
> **D.** Other (please specify)?
>
> Any feedback helps me serve you better.
>
> Best,
> Leo

## 制造紧迫感（季前/节日前）

**中文说明**：在圣诞/Prime Day/夏季前1-2个月发，强调原材料涨价和交期紧张。

**Subject:** Pre-[Christmas/Prime Day] stock update

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Given the upcoming [Christmas season / Prime Day / Q4] and raw material price trends, our current price for [Product] is valid until [Date]. We also have limited pre-season stock for quick shipment.
>
> Shall I reserve some capacity for your order?
>
> Best,
> Leo

## 序列执行要点

| 封数 | 时机 | 角度 | 核心目的 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | 个性化开场 | 开话题 |
| 2 | Day 3 | 简单bump | 防淹没 |
| 3 | Day 7 | 给物料 | 降门槛 |
| 4 | Day 14 | 场景案例 | 建适配感 |
| 5 | Day 21 | 参数对比 | 降判断成本 |
| 6 | Day 30 | 商务方案 | 推进成交 |
| 7 | Day 30+ | Breakup | 判断生死 |

> **注意**：如果客户在中间任何一封回复了，立即停止序列，转入正常询盘沟通。
