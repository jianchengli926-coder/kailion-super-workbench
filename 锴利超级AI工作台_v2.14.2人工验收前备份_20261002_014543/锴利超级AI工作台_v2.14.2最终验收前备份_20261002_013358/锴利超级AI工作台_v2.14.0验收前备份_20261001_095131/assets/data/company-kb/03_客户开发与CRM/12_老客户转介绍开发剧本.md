# 实战剧本12：老客户转介绍开发剧本

> **用途**：让满意的老客户帮你介绍新客户。转介绍来的线索信任度最高、成交最快、价格抗性最低。
> **核心心法**：转介绍不是"求"来的，是在客户**最满意的那个瞬间**顺势开口，并给一个让他愿意开口的理由。
> ⚠️ 所有折扣/佣金/样品/数量为示例数据，非公司实际政策。客户用 Client A/B/C 代称。

---

## 一、转介绍的黄金时机（When to Ask）

| 时机 | 为什么有效 | 开口方式 |
|---|---|---|
| 客户确认收到货、说"质量很好"时 | 满意度峰值 | 顺势一句 |
| 客户复购下单后 | 已经用钱包投票 | 邮件/电话 |
| 客户主动夸你/感谢你时 | 情绪高点 | 立刻接话 |
| 客户在社媒晒图/好评时 | 公开认可 | 私信感谢+请求 |
| 年度对账/节日问候时 | 关系维护节点 | 软性请求 |

> ❌ 错误时机：客户刚抱怨完、货还没到、价格谈崩时——**绝对不要**这时开口。

---

## 二、转介绍请求话术（英文邮件 + 电话）

### 2.1 邮件模板（客户复购后发送）

**Subject**：Loved working with you — know anyone else?

```
Hi [Client A name],

Great news — your repeat order just shipped yesterday. Thank you again for trusting us with your business.

I wanted to ask a small favor. If you know any fellow importers, distributors, or brand owners who are looking for a reliable knife supplier, I'd be grateful if you could introduce us.

As a thank-you, for every referral that places a first order, I'd love to offer:
- You: [示例: 5%] discount on your next order, or free samples
- Your contact: [示例: 3%] off their first order

No pressure at all — only if it feels natural. Either way, thank you for being such a great partner.

Best regards,
Leo Li
KaiLionCrafts
```

### 2.2 电话/WhatsApp 口播脚本（更自然）

> **You**: Hey [Client A name], just wanted to confirm your last order arrived in good shape.
> **Client A**: Yes, everything's perfect. The customer feedback has been great.
> **You**: That's awesome to hear, thank you. Actually — I was wondering, do you know other buyers in [their country] who might need similar quality? We'd take good care of them, same as we do you.
> **Client A**: Actually, I have a friend who runs a kitchenware store…
> **You**: That's wonderful. If you're open to it, you could just forward my email, or I can send you a short intro note to pass along.

### 2.3 客户表达满意时的即时话术（现场/消息）

> "That means a lot, thank you. If you ever meet someone looking for knives, we'd really appreciate the introduction — and I'll make sure they get the same (or better) treatment you do."

## 三、转介绍激励机制（Referral Incentives · 示例）

| 激励类型 | 给老客户（推荐人） | 给被介绍人 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 折扣型 | 下一单 [示例: 5%] off | 首单 [示例: 3%] off | 最常用，双方都爽 |
| 样品型 | 免费新款样品一套 | — | 适合新品推广 |
| 佣金型 | 首单金额 [示例: 2%] 返点 | — | 适合大 B 客户，谨慎使用 |
| 非物质 | 优先打样/优先排产/季度新品预览 | — | 关系型客户更吃这套 |

> ⚠️ 佣金/返点涉及合规与税务，大额激励需财务确认后再承诺。示例数据仅供参考。

## 四、被介绍客户的首次接触话术

### 4.1 邮件（老客户已转达联系方式）

**Subject**：Introduced by [Client A name] — knife supplier

```
Hi [Client B name],

I hope this message finds you well. [Client A name] suggested I reach out — he's been working with us for [示例: 2 years] and thought we might be a good fit for your business.

We're KaiLionCrafts, a Yangjiang-based manufacturer of kitchen knives, shears, and BBQ tools, exporting to [示例: 75] countries.

Would it be okay if I sent over our catalog and a quick company profile? No obligation — just wanted to say hello.

Best regards,
Leo Li
KaiLionCrafts
P.S. — [Client A name] sends his regards!
```

### 4.2 首次通话开场（如果是电话/WhatsApp）

> "Hi, is this [Client B]? My name is Leo from KaiLionCrafts. [Client A] — who you know in [city] — mentioned you might be looking for a knife supplier. He's been our customer for [示例: 2 years]. Is now an okay time for a quick chat?"

> 关键：**第一句就报出老客户名字**，信任瞬间建立，回复率比冷开发高 3–5 倍。

## 五、完整英文对话示例（转介绍闭环）

```
【老客户 Client A，复购后】

You: Hi [Client A], your reorder shipped today. Tracking number inside.

Client A: Perfect, thanks. Customers love the chef knives.

You: That's great to hear, really. Quick question — do you know any other
     distributors in Germany who might need reliable supply? I'd take
     good care of them.

Client A: Actually, my supplier friend [Client B] in Munich has been
         complaining about his current knife quality.

You: Oh, I'd love to help. Would you mind introducing us? I can send
    you a short intro email to forward, and as a thank-you I'll add
    [示例: 5%] off your next order.

Client A: Sure, send it over.

【You → Client B】（见 4.1 邮件模板）

Client B: Hi Leo, yes [Client A] mentioned you. Send the catalog.

You: Sent! Based on what [Client A] told me about your store, I've
    picked out 3 bestseller SKUs. Shall I arrange samples?

Client B: Sounds good.

【3 周后】
Client B: Samples arrived. Quality is good. What's your MOQ?

You: MOQ is [示例: 500 pcs] per SKU. Here's the quotation.

【1 个月后】Client B 首单成交

You: Hi [Client A], good news — [Client B] just placed his first order.
    As promised, [示例: 5%] off your next order is on the books.
    Thank you for the trust!
```

## 六、转介绍跟踪与感谢流程

1. **登记**：每一条转介绍线索在 CRM 标注来源 = "Referral by Client A"（字段见 `16_CRM字段定义与数据规范.md`）。
2. **24h 内**：给老客户发一封感谢信（即使被介绍人还没回复）。
3. **被介绍人成交后**：立刻兑现激励（折扣/样品/返点），并**再次**书面感谢老客户。
4. **月度复盘**：统计"老客户转介绍成交数"，对最活跃的推荐人额外送一份感谢礼。
5. **闭环反馈**：告诉老客户"你介绍的人成交了"——这会激励他介绍下一个。
