## 教程 1 · 厨刀保养（Chef Knife Care）

### 基本信息
| 项目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 目标观众 | 刚收到主厨刀/三德刀/小菜刀的终端用户 |
| 时长 | 90 秒 |
| 平台 | YouTube / Product Page / 售后邮件 |
| 拍摄地点 | 厨房台面，浅色大理石砧板 |

### 分镜表

| 镜号 | 时长 | 画面 | VO (EN) | 字幕 (EN) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Hook | 0–5s | 一把好刀切番茄，再切一把生锈的钝刀切不动 | *"Want your KaiLionCrafts knife to last 10 years? Do these four things."* | Make your knife last 10 years |
| 2 · Clean | 5–25s | 温水 + 洗洁精手洗刀，软布擦干；特写"别放洗碗机"红叉 | *"Hand-wash with warm soapy water. Never put it in the dishwasher — the heat and detergent ruin the edge and the handle."* | ① Hand-wash only · No dishwasher |
| 3 · Dry & Oil | 25–45s | 软布擦干 → 刀尖抹一层薄矿物油 | *"Dry it immediately. For long-term storage, wipe a thin layer of food-safe mineral oil on the blade — it prevents rust."* | ② Dry immediately · Oil for storage |
| 4 · Sharpen | 45–70s | 磨刀石 1000 目/3000 目，15° 角度推拉，发出刺耳沙沙声 | *"Hone it every use on a steel. Sharpen on a whetstone every 2–3 months — hold the edge at 15 degrees, 10 passes each side."* | ③ Hone weekly · Sharpen every 2–3 months @ 15° |
| 5 · Store | 70–85s | 刀架 / 磁贴 / 刀套入鞘；特写"别抽屉乱塞"红叉 | *"Store it on a magnetic strip, in a knife block, or in a sheath. Throwing it loose in a drawer dulls the edge."* | ④ Block / magnet / sheath · Not loose in drawer |
| 6 · CTA | 85–90s | 产品 + 品牌 Logo | *"Care for your knife, and it'll care for your cooking."* | KaiLionCrafts · Knives that last |

### 拍摄要点
- 磨刀画面必须拍角度特写（用手机量角器 App 辅助）；
- "洗碗机"镜头用红色大叉叠加，记忆点强；
- 全程 ASMR 收音（水声、磨刀沙沙声）。

## 教程 2 · 剪刀使用与保养（Kitchen Shears Care）

### 基本信息
| 项目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 目标观众 | 买了厨房剪/多功能剪的用户 |
| 时长 | 60 秒 |
| 痛点 | 剪几下就松、生锈、剪不动鸡骨 |

### 分镜表

| 镜号 | 时长 | 画面 | VO (EN) | 字幕 (EN) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Hook | 0–5s | 一刀剪开鸡骨的慢动作 | *"Kitchen shears that actually cut through bone."* | Real bone-cutting shears |
| 2 · Adjust Tension | 5–20s | 特写枢轴螺丝：用硬币拧紧/拧松；剪一张纸测试 | *"Loose shears tear instead of cut. Tighten the pivot screw with a coin until the blades close smoothly but resist a little."* | Adjust tension with a coin |
| 3 · Disassemble & Clean | 20–35s | 双手掰开剪刀（可拆卸设计），冲水，软布擦干 | *"Ours come apart for cleaning. Rinse between the blades after cutting raw meat — that's where gunk hides."* | Take apart · Clean between blades |
| 4 · Lubricate | 35–48s | 在枢轴滴一滴矿物油，开合几次 | *"One drop of food-safe oil at the pivot every month keeps the action smooth."* | 1 drop of oil / month |
| 5 · Don'ts | 48–55s | 红叉：剪金属线、剪纸张以外的硬物、放洗碗机 | *"Don't cut wire, don't cut thick plastic, and never put them in the dishwasher."* | Don't: wire · plastic · dishwasher |
| 6 · CTA | 55–60s | 产品英雄镜头 | *"One pair. Chicken bones, twine, packaging — done."* | KaiLionCrafts Shears |

### 拍摄要点
- "剪开鸡骨"慢动作是核心爆点，240fps；
- 可拆卸结构要拍清楚（用户买前最关心）。

## 教程 3 · 户外刀维护（Outdoor / EDC Knife Care）

### 基本信息
| 项目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 目标观众 | 买了户外刀/直刀/EDC 的用户 |
| 时长 | 90 秒 |
| 合规提醒 | 开头口播 "For bushcraft and camping use only — not a weapon." |

### 分镜表

| 镜号 | 时长 | 画面 | VO (EN) | 字幕 (EN) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Hook | 0–5s | 户外场景：刀削木屑、切绳、开箱 | *"For bushcraft and camping use only. Here's how to keep your fixed-blade ready for the trail."* | For bushcraft / camping use |
| 2 · Field Clean | 5–25s | 户外用清水/矿泉水冲刀，纸巾擦干，抹一点蜡 | *"After every trip, wipe off sap and dirt. At camp, rinse with water and dry thoroughly — sap traps moisture and causes rust."* | Clean after every trip |
| 3 · Edge Maintenance | 25–50s | 户外随身磨刀棒（pocket sharpener）拉几下；回家后上磨刀石 | *"Touch up the edge with a pocket sharpener in the field. At home, restore it on a 1000-grit stone at 20 degrees."* | Field: pocket steel · Home: 1000-grit @ 20° |
| 4 · Rust Prevention | 50–65s | 刀身抹枪油/矿物油，Kydex 鞘内喷蜡 | *"Carbon steel rusts fast. Wipe a thin coat of oil after every use. Spray the sheath with dry wax to absorb moisture."* | Oil the blade · Wax the sheath |
| 5 · Sheath Care | 65–78s | Kydex 鞘冲洗、阴干；皮鞘上貂油 | *"Kydex: rinse and air-dry. Leather: condition with leather oil twice a year."* | Kydex: rinse · Leather: condition |
| 6 · Safety | 78–85s | 刀入鞘、腰带固定、不抛扔 | *"Always sheath it. Never throw it. Respect the edge."* | Sheath it. Respect the edge. |
| 7 · CTA | 85–90s | 产品 + Kydex 鞘英雄镜头 | *"Built for the wild. Built to last."* | KaiLionCrafts EDC |

### 拍摄要点
- 真户外场景（树林/营地）拍，不要在绿幕前；
- 开头必须口播合规声明；
- 不出现刀刃对人、投掷、危险动作。
