### ARTICLE 9 — INSURANCE

- Under **CIF** terms: Insurance to be covered by Seller for 110% of invoice value against All Risks.
- Under **FOB / EXW** terms: Insurance to be covered by Buyer.

---

### ARTICLE 10 — QUALITY STANDARD

1. Products shall conform to the confirmed sample / specifications provided by buyer.
2. Three-round quality control:
   - Incoming raw material inspection (steel hardness, composition, dimensions)
   - In-process production inspection (heat treatment monitoring, edge consistency, assembly)
   - Pre-shipment video confirmation (buyer's written approval required)
3. Certifications available upon request: CE / FDA / LFGB / RoHS / ISO 9001 / BSCI / FSC.
4. Leo Li personally signs off every shipment.

---

### ARTICLE 11 — INSPECTION AND CLAIMS

1. **Pre-shipment Inspection:** Seller will send video/photos of finished goods for buyer's confirmation before shipment. Buyer should reply within 3 working days; otherwise deemed approved.
2. **Quality Claim:** Should the quality of goods be found not in conformity with the contract, buyer shall lodge a claim within **30 days** after arrival of goods at destination port, supported by an inspection report issued by a recognized inspection agency.
3. Seller shall, depending on the case, lodge replacement, reduction in price, or refuse the goods.
4. Claims in respect of matters within the responsibility of insurance company or shipping company will not be considered by seller.

---

### ARTICLE 12 — FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party shall be held responsible for failure or delay to perform all or any part of this contract due to flood, fire, earthquake, typhoon, war, pandemic, government regulation, or other unforeseeable events that are unavoidable and insurmountable.
The affected party shall notify the other party within **7 days** and provide official certification within **15 days**.
Both parties shall then negotiate whether to cancel the contract, delay the performance, or partially exempt the performance.

---

### ARTICLE 13 — ARBITRATION

All disputes arising from the execution of this contract shall be settled through friendly negotiation.
In case no settlement can be reached, the case shall be submitted to:
- **China International Economic and Trade Arbitration Commission (CIETAC)**, Shenzhen Sub-Commission, for arbitration in accordance with its arbitral rules. The arbitral award is final and binding upon both parties.
- *Or:* `[Buyer's preferred arbitration venue, e.g. Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC)]`

The arbitration fee shall be borne by the losing party.

---

### ARTICLE 14 — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CONFIDENTIALITY

1. Buyer retains all rights to its trademarks, designs, and proprietary information provided to Seller for production.
2. Seller shall not disclose buyer's designs, customer information, or order details to any third party.
3. Seller signs NDA prior to disclosure of buyer's proprietary designs.
4. Buyer shall not reverse-engineer Seller's manufacturing processes.

---

### ARTICLE 15 — WARRANTY

1. **Lifetime Warranty:** Against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.
2. **10-Year Blade Guarantee:** Edge retention for blades made of VG10 / 9Cr18MoV / Damascus / 1.4116 steel.
3. **2-Year Assembly Warranty:** Against defects in handle, rivets, and pin assembly.
4. Warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, misuse, abuse, or accidental damage.

---

1. This contract comes into effect upon signing and stamping by both parties.
2. Amendments to this contract shall be made in writing and agreed by both parties.
3. This contract is made in two originals, one for each party.
4. PI (Proforma Invoice) No. `[PI number]` is an integral part of this contract.
5. All communications shall be in English via email.
