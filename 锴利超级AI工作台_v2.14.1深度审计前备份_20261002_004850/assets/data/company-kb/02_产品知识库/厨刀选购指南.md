## 四、选购要点（Key Buying Criteria）

### 1. 刃角（Edge Angle）
- **日式 15°±1°/侧**：本公司标准阳江水磨开刃，锋利度高，适合切菜切肉；对应 SS-012/DS 系列
- **西式 20°/侧**：刃口更壮，适合重型切配；对应 SS-016（德式锻造）
- **给客户建议**：家用选 15° 更锋利；餐饮后厨高频使用可选 20° 更耐造

### 2. 硬度 HRC
- **55–58 HRC**：易磨、防锈好，家用足够
- **57–59 HRC**：5Cr15MoV 主力区间，平衡之选
- **60–62 HRC**：高端单刀，保持好但磨起来稍费力
- **65–67 HRC（VG10 芯）**：收藏/专业级，建议配专业磨刀服务
- ⚠️ 本库多数 SKU 实测 HRC 待工厂确认，以 QC 报告为准

### 3. 手柄材质
| 柄材 | 优点 | 适合场景 | 代表 SKU |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pakkawood 树脂浸渍木 | 耐水稳定、主流耐看 | 全场景主力 | SS-012 / DS-016 |
| G10 Fiberglass | 防滑耐水、EDC/战术感 | 现代厨房、FBA | DS-018 |
| Rosewood 红木 | 天然纹理、中式美学 | 中式菜刀、礼品 | SS-003 / SS-016 |
| POM 聚甲醛 | 耐油耐洗、洗碗机友好 | 餐饮后厨 | SS-018 |
| Abalone Shell 鲍鱼壳 | 每把独一无二、收藏级 | 高端礼品 | DS-001 / DS-017 |
| Walnut 胡桃木 | 高级感、日式美学 | 锤纹高端线 | HM-001 |

### 4. 平衡（Balance）
- **Full Tang 全龙骨**：刀钢贯穿手柄，平衡好、强度高；本库高端款均为 Full Tang
- **锻造刀枕配重**：SS-016 锻造款刀枕处偏厚，手感沉稳，适合西式切配
- **给客户建议**：一把好厨刀平衡点应在刀枕前方 1–2cm，切菜不压手

### 5. 维护（Maintenance）
- **不锈钢款（SS 系列）**：可洗碗机（以具体报告为准），日常擦干即可
- **Damascus / 高碳钢（DS/HC 系列）**：⚠️ **不可洗碗机**；用后擦干、定期涂矿物油；会形成 patina（包浆）属正常现象
- **磨刀建议**：15° 刃口用 15° 定角磨刀器；VG10/Damascus 建议专业磨刀

## 六、常见误区（Common Myths）

1. **"越硬越好（HRC 越高越好）"**
   → 错。HRC 太高（>62）刀刃会变脆，容易崩口。家用 57–59 是甜区；VG10 芯 65–67 虽高但有软芯钢缓冲，且需专业维护。客户如果只是日常切菜，5Cr15MoV 完全够用。

2. **"大马士革钢一定比普通不锈钢好"**
   → 半对半错。真 Damascus（VG10 芯多层焊合）确实性能+颜值双高，但要注意区分：**真 Damascus 花纹是多层钢材焊接后酸蚀出的纹路**；有些低价款只是在不锈钢表面**酸蚀仿纹**（如 DS-008/DS-018 待工厂确认）。对客描述时必须如实说明。

3. **"中式菜刀就是砍骨刀"**
   → 错。中式菜刀分**切片刀**（刃薄，切菜切肉）和**斩骨刀**（刃厚 ≥4mm，斩骨）。本库 SS-001/003 是切片刀风格；真要斩骨需选斩切款（刃厚待工厂确认）。英文里不要叫 "Bone Knife"，叫 "Chinese Cleaver"。

4. **"不锈钢刀永远不会生锈"**
   → 错。所有马氏体不锈钢（包括 5Cr15MoV、440C）在长期潮湿/接触盐醋后仍会锈。只是比高碳钢好很多。建议客户用后擦干，别泡水槽。

5. **"Full Tang 一定比 Partial Tang 好"**
   → 绝大多数情况是，但 Partial Tang 设计得当也能很耐用。对家用而言 Full Tang 确实更安心；不过很多日式薄刃厨刀用 Partial Tang 是为了减轻重量——这是设计选择，不是偷工减料。

6. **"刀越贵越锋利"**
   → 错。锋利度主要取决于**开刃角度和研磨工艺**，不是钢材牌子。一把 5Cr15MoV 15° 开刃的阳江刀，可以比某大牌 20° 开刃的刀更锋利。我们的卖点就是"阳江水磨 15° 开刃"。

7. **"木柄一定比合成柄好"**
   → 看场景。木柄（红木/胡桃木）颜值高、手感好，但需保养怕泡水；Pakkawood/G10/POM 耐水耐洗、洗碗机友好，餐饮后厨反而更实用。家用选木柄，商用选 POM/Pakkawood。

8. **"可以用洗碗机洗所有刀"**
   → 错。Damascus、高碳钢手锻款**绝对不能洗碗机**（会锈、会伤花纹）；不锈钢款虽然很多可洗碗机，但长期高温高压仍会加速柄材老化。建议手洗擦干。

## 七、英文客户问答（English Customer Q&A）

**Q1: What's the best chef knife for everyday home cooking?**
A: For most home cooks, we recommend our 8-inch chef knife in 5Cr15MoV stainless steel at 15° per side — that's the KL-KN-SS-012. It hits the sweet spot of sharpness, rust resistance, and price. If you're on a tighter budget, the KL-KN-SS-015 (German-style, walnut handle) offers the lowest entry cost.

**Q2: What's the difference between 5Cr15MoV and VG10 Damascus?**
A: 5Cr15MoV is our workhorse stainless at 57–59 HRC — affordable, easy to maintain, perfect for daily use. VG10 Damascus uses a VG10 core (65–67 HRC) clad in 67 layers of stainless steel — it holds an edge far longer and has the distinctive wave pattern, but it costs more and needs a bit more care (hand-wash, occasional oiling).

**Q3: Are these knives dishwasher safe?**
A: Our stainless-steel everyday knives (SS series) are generally dishwasher-friendly, but we recommend hand-washing and drying for longest life. Damascus and hand-forged high-carbon knives (DS and HC series) should NEVER go in the dishwasher — hand-wash, dry immediately, and oil occasionally to prevent rust.

**Q4: Do you offer OEM / private label? What's the MOQ?**
A: Yes. Light customization (laser logo, handle choice, custom packaging) starts at 50–150 pcs with a ~15-day lead time. Full ODM (custom blade shape, new mold) starts at 500 pcs. Standard MOQ per single SKU is 100 pcs; mixed containers across knives, scissors, and kitchen tools are welcome.

**Q5: What certifications do your knives have?**
A: Our main factory holds SGS LFGB food-contact compliance (Germany), US FDA 21 CFR 109.6 lead-migration pass, SGS 21-item heavy-metal migration all ND per EN 13130, and DIN 10955 sensory test pass. Mill certificates (EN 10204-3.1) are available on request.

**Q6: What is Full Tang and why does it matter?**
A: Full Tang means the steel blade runs continuously through the handle, with scales riveted on both sides. It gives superior balance, strength, and durability compared to partial-tang or stamped knives. All our premium chef knives are Full Tang construction.

**Q7: How do I care for a Damascus knife?**
A: Wipe the blade dry after every use — never leave it wet or in the sink. Do not put it in the dishwasher. Over time Damascus and high-carbon steels develop a natural patina (a thin darkening) — this is normal and actually protects the blade, it does NOT mean it's rusty. Apply a light coat of food-safe mineral oil every few weeks. Store on a magnetic strip or in a knife block, not loose in a drawer.

**Q8: Can I get samples before placing a bulk order?**
A: Yes. Sample lead time is 10–15 days. Sample fees are payable via PayPal or Western Union and are refundable/deductible from your bulk order. We also provide pre-production 3D modeling (within 7 days for full ODM) and pre-shipment video confirmation.
