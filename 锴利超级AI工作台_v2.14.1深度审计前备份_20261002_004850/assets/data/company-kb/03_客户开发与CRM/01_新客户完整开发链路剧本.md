## Round 8 — 我方谈判应对（不降价，先摆差异）

> Hi Hans,
>
> I completely understand that price is a key factor — and I appreciate you being direct.
>
> Before I adjust anything, may I ask a few questions so I'm comparing apples to apples:
>
> 1. What hardness (HRC) does your current supplier guarantee? Ours is **HRC 56-58**, tested at our partner lab. Many 5Cr15MoV knives on the market run HRC 52-54, which means they dull 2-3× faster.
> 2. Is their blade **full tang** or half tang? Ours is full tang (see the spec sheet — the steel runs the full length of the handle).
> 3. Does their price include a **pre-shipment video approval** and a **10-year blade guarantee**?
>
> That said, I don't want price to be the only reason we don't work together. Here are two options I can offer:
>
> **Option A — Keep the spec, increase volume:** If you can raise the order to **1,500 pcs**, I can bring the unit price down to **$6.90** (logo included), which is very close to your current cost.
>
> **Option B — Keep 800 pcs, adjust the spec:** We can switch to our **KL-KN-SS-015** (8" German steel chef knife, walnut handle, high-carbon stainless) at **$5.80/pc FOB for 800 pcs** — different handle aesthetic, same LFGB-certified blade.
>
> My recommendation: start with **Option A at 1,500 pcs**. It locks in near-your-current-price and gives you a genuine second source.
>
> Best,
> Leo

## Round 15 — 我方回复反馈 + 发出正式PI

> Hi Hans,
>
> Great feedback — thank you and your team for the thorough test.
>
> On your two questions:
> 1. **Rosewood color variation**: Natural wood has natural grain variation, which is normal.
