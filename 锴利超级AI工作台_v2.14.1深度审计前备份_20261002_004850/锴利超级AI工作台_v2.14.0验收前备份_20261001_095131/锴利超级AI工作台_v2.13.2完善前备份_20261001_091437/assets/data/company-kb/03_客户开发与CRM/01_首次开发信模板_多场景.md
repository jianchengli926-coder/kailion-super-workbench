# 首次开发信模板（多场景）

> **使用说明**：以下模板均为英文，直接发给海外客户。
> 每封模板前有中文使用说明。`[方括号]`内为需替换的变量。
> 原则：150词以内，三短段，个性化开头 + 1个痛点 + 轻量CTA。

---

## 场景1：通用版（适用于所有未知类型客户）

**中文说明**：最常用版本，不知道客户类型时用这封。开头提及客户网站具体产品，中间给1个价值点，结尾轻量CTA。

**Subject Line Options:**
- `[Your Company] - Direct Factory Cutlery from Yangjiang`
- `Knives & Scissors Manufacturer - 20% Below Trading Company Price`
- `Saw your store - quick question about your cutlery sourcing`

**Email Body:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I was browsing [Client Website] and noticed your [specific product category, e.g. kitchen knife collection] — really impressed with [specific detail, e.g. the Damascus series / your packaging design].
>
> I'm Leo from KaiLionCrafts, a direct cutlery manufacturer based in Yangjiang, China — the city that produces 75% of China's knives and scissors. We supply kitchen knives, scissors, outdoor knives and kitchen tools to brands and importers in [client's region]. Our products are SGS-tested to LFGB and FDA standards.
>
> Would you be open to a 15-minute call this week, or should I send our latest catalog and price list first?
>
> Best regards,
> Leo Li
> KaiLionCrafts | Yangjiang Kaili International Trading Co., Ltd.
> WhatsApp: +86 131-3800-6564
> Web: kailioncrafts.com

## 场景2：针对进口商（Importer/Distributor）

**中文说明**：发给有真实进口记录的进口商/分销商。强调工厂直供、稳定供货、可做贴牌。

**Subject Line:**
- `Direct factory pricing for your cutlery import program`
- `Alternative supplier for [Client Brand] - factory direct from Yangjiang`

**Email Body:**

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I noticed [Client Company] has been importing cutlery from China (per customs records). We're a direct factory in Yangjiang working with 4 specialized manufacturing plants.
>
> What we offer:
> - Factory-direct pricing (typically 20% below trading companies)
> - Stable monthly capacity for repeat orders
> - OEM/ODM with your logo and packaging
> - SGS LFGB + FDA certified products
>
> Would it make sense to send our catalog and a sample range so you can compare quality and pricing? Samples can be sent DHL today.
>
> Best regards,
> Leo Li
> KaiLionCrafts

**中文说明**：广交会/行业展会上交换名片后48小时内发出。提及展会上聊过的具体产品。

**Subject Line:**

## 通用注意事项

1. **发送时间**：按目标国时区定时发送（见《10_全球时区与最佳联系时间速查表》）
2. **邮箱**：发个人邮箱（peter@xxx.com），别发info@
3. **签名**：必须含姓名、职位、WhatsApp、网站
4. **附件**：首封别发大附件，用Google Drive链接或说"can send on request"
5. **个性化**：每封必须改开头那句，证明不是群发
6. **字数**：控制在150词以内，手机一屏看完
