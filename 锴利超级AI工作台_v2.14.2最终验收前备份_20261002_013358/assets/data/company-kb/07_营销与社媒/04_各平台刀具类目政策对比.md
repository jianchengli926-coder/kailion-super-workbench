## 2. Geographic / Legal Restrictions / 各州各国法规差异

### 2.1 United States — Federal

- **Federal Switchblade Act (1958, amended 2009)**: Bans interstate shipment of switchblades **except** for states where legal, and except for military/government use. In practice, marketplaces enforce stricter-than-federal bans.

### 2.2 United States — State Highlights

| State | Key Rule | Source |
|-------|----------|--------|
| **California** | Switchblade ≥2" blade = misdemeanor (Cal. Pen. Code §21510); 9th Cir. upheld ban Jan 2026. Butterfly/gravity treated as switchblade. Own at home OK, but selling/shipping into CA restricted. | californiapublic.law / Knife Rights v. Bonta |
| **New York** | Gravity knife criminal ban repealed 2019; **NYC** still bans public carry of blades >4". | urbanedc.com state guide |
| **Texas / Florida / Ohio** | Permitless carry of most knives; very knife-friendly. | - |
| **Massachusetts** | Folding knives must not lock (no lock-blade carry) in some interpretations. | - |
| **New Jersey** | Butterfly/gravity banned; blade length limits. | - |

> **运营结论**: Kitchen knives are legal in all 50 states. Manual folding knives need geo-block checks. Never list automatic/OTF/balisong on US marketplaces.

### 2.3 United Kingdom

- **Offensive Weapons Act 2019** + Criminal Justice Act 1988: bans sale of flick knives, gravity knives, butterfly knives, stealth knives, zombie knives, push daggers, belt-buckle knives.
- **Age 18+** sale mandatory; Online Age Verification (OAV) enforced by Amazon/eBay.
- Folding pocket knives ≤3" (7.62 cm) are legal to sell to adults.
- Kitchen knives: legal to sell, but **eBay UK bans them from listing**; Amazon UK allows with age flag.

### 2.2 European Union (Germany as reference)

- **German Waffengesetz (WaffG)**: bans Springmesser (switchblades) regardless of blade length; bans fixed-blade knives with one-handed opening. Folding knives with **locked blade + one-handed opening** also restricted.
- General EU: blade length >11-12 cm restricted in DE/FR/ES.
- Age 18+ sale throughout EU.

### 2.4 Australia

- Almost all fixed-blade and assisted-opening knives banned except genuine kitchen/cutting tools.
- Butterfly, switchblade, OTF, push knives fully banned.
- Age 18+; strict import controls.

## 3. Compliance Sales Checklist / 合规销售清单

### 3.1 Product Listing

- [ ] Product categorized as kitchen/utility/camping tool — **never** weapon/tactical/self-defense.
- [ ] Title, bullets, backend keywords free of: switchblade, automatic, OTF, stiletto, tactical, combat, zombie, military, self-defense.
- [ ] Blade length honestly disclosed in Item Specifics.
- [ ] Opening mechanism honestly declared (Manual / Assisted / Auto).

### 3.2 Images

- [ ] No blood, no stabbing motion, no tactical/military background.
- [ ] Include blade guard / sheath / blade cover in packaging photo.
- [ ] Ruler or coin in photo for scale.

### 3.3 Packaging & Shipping

- [ ] Blade covered with plastic sheath or cardboard edge guard.
- [ ] Outer box plain, no "knife" labeling visible (reduces theft).
- [ ] Age 18+ warning label on inner packaging.
- [ ] Signature confirmation required on delivery (US/UK).
- [ ] Geo-block: ship only to states/countries where SKU is legal.

### 3.4 Customer Service

- [ ] Order confirmation email includes: "This product is sold as a kitchen/utility tool. Buyer must be 18+."
- [ ] No sales to minors; cancel orders with suspicious billing addresses (PO boxes near schools).

## 4. Account Ban Risks & Avoidance / 封号风险与规避

### 4.1 Highest-Risk Behaviors

1. Listing an automatic/switchblade on Amazon/eBay/Walmart → immediate listing removal, possible account suspension.
2. Using "tactical" / "self-defense" keywords on Walmart → automatic unpublish + warning.
3. Shipping a banned knife into UK/AU without geo-block → customs seizure + account strike.
4. Sourcing fake UPC / brand names → IP claim → account deactivation.

### 4.2 Appeal Playbook

- If listing is taken down for "weapons": respond with (a) product photos showing kitchen use, (b) manufacturer declaration that product is a culinary tool, (c) US state-law legality letter.
- Never re-list the same ASIN/GTIN under a different title — that escalates to suspension.
