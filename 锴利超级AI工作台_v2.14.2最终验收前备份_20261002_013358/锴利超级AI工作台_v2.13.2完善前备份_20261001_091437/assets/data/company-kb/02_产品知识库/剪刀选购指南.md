## 三、结构选择（By Structure）

### 1. 可拆卸 vs 不可拆卸
| 类型 | 优点 | 缺点 | 代表 SKU |
|---|---|---|---|
| **可拆卸（Detachable）** | 可彻底清洗、洗碗机友好、卫生 | 结构略复杂 | KL-SC-PR-011 / PR-012 |
| **一体不可拆卸** | 结构强度最高、剪骨最有力 | 清洁死角 | KL-SC-SS-007（重型一体） |

> **给客户建议**：食品接触卫生敏感 → 可拆卸（PR-011）；要剪禽骨重型 → 一体（SS-007）。

### 2. 张力调节（Tension Adjustment）
- **可调中心螺丝**：专业款标配，用户可自行调节剪切手感；理发剪 HC-002 标配
- **固定中心铆钉**：走量款，免维护但手感不可调；SS-005 等入门款
- ⚠️ 逐 SKU 张力方式待工厂确认

### 3. 刃口磨型
| 磨型 | 特点 | 用途 | 代表 SKU |
|---|---|---|---|
| **平磨（Flat Grind）** | 干净利落 | 裁缝剪、园艺剪 | HC-001/003、PR-002/003 |
| **凹磨（Hollow Grind）** | 剪切顺滑不卡 | 理发剪 | KL-SC-HC-002 |
| **锯齿刃（Serrated）** | 防滑咬食材/滑物 | 厨房剪、禽肉剪 | PR-004/006/007、SS-005/006 |
| **弯刃（Curved）** | 绕骨剪切 | 家禽拆解 | KL-SC-SS-008 |

### 4. 旁路式 vs 砧式（厨房剪结构）
- **旁路式（Bypass）**：两片刃交错滑过，像普通剪刀——精细剪切、食材剪；本库多数厨房剪
- **砧式（Anvil）**：一片刃贴平砧面，压切——重型剪骨、省力；对应 SS-007 重型一体
- ⚠️ 具体结构逐 SKU 待工厂确认

## 四、选购要点（Key Buying Criteria）

### 1. 刃长（Blade Length / 全长）
- **厨房剪**：20–25cm 全长（主力）
- **裁缝剪**：8–11 inch（20–28cm），专业款选 10–11 inch
- **理发剪**：6.5 inch（16.5cm）标准
- **园艺剪**：20–30cm
- ⚠️ 具体刃长 mm 待工厂确认

### 2. 磨型（Grind）
- 剪布要平磨镜面（不抽丝）
- 剪头发要凹磨（不卡发）
- 剪食材要锯齿（防滑）

### 3. 张力（Tension）
- 专业剪必须可调螺丝，否则用久了松垮
- 走量款固定铆钉即可

### 4. 手柄（Handle）
| 柄材 | 优点 | 代表 |
|---|---|---|
| 全不锈钢一体 | 无塑料件、耐用卫生 | PR-004/006/007、HC-001 |
| PP+TPR 软胶双色 | 防滑舒适、走量 | SS-005/006/009 |
| PP 塑料（白/黑） | 轻便、园艺/花艺 | SS-003/004 |
| 钢丝环 | 干净利落、不藏污 | PR-013 |

### 5. 中心螺丝 / 铆钉
- 可调螺丝 = 专业款
- 固定铆钉 = 走量款
- 用久了松动 → 紧螺丝或更换

## 六、常见误区（Common Myths）

1. **"厨房剪和普通剪刀一样"**
   → 错。厨房剪需要锯齿刃（防滑咬湿滑食材）、可拆卸清洗（食品卫生）、常带开瓶器/夹核桃多功能。普通办公剪剪鸡骨会崩口。

2. **"越贵的剪刀越锋利"**
   → 错。锋利度取决于磨型和钢材热处理，不是价格。3Cr13 走量剪磨得好也很利；关键是用对场景——理发剪不能剪布，厨房剪不能剪铁丝。

3. **"全钢剪一定比塑料柄好"**
   → 看场景。全钢剪耐用卫生、可洗碗机，但冬天冰手、摔了容易砸坏台面；PP+TPR 软胶柄防滑舒适、走量便宜。餐饮后厨选全钢，家用选软胶柄。

4. **"弹簧剪刀只是省力噱头"**
   → 不是。长时间 BBQ 或花店高频使用，弹簧自动张开能显著减少手部疲劳（扳机指）。这是功能性设计，不是噱头。

5. **"可拆卸结构会松动"**
   → 优质可拆卸厨房剪用可调中心螺丝+定位销，锁紧后不会松；反而因为能拆开彻底清洗，寿命更长。缺点是一体剪剪骨绝对强度更高——重型禽骨选一体（SS-007）。

6. **"锯齿刃不如平磨锋利"**
   → 错。锯齿刃和平磨用途不同：平磨剪布干净，锯齿剪食材防滑。剪湿滑的鸡皮/香草，锯齿比平磨好用得多。

7. **"剪刀不需要保养"**
   → 错。用后擦干（尤其剪酸性食材）、定期紧中心螺丝、枢轴点偶尔上油。理发剪建议送专业磨刀服务。

8. **"阳江剪刀都是廉价货"**
   → 错。阳江占全球不锈钢剪刀 >70% 产能，本库从 $1.2 走量款到 5Cr15MoV 专业款全覆盖，且主厂有 SGS LFGB + FDA 认证。产地是卖点不是贬义词。

## 七、英文客户问答（English Customer Q&A）

**Q1: What's the difference between kitchen shears and regular scissors?**
A: Kitchen shears are purpose-built: they usually have serrated blades to grip slippery food (poultry, herbs), many are detachable for thorough cleaning, and they often include built-in tools like a bottle opener or nutcracker. Regular office scissors will chip or dull quickly if you cut bones or tough food with them. We recommend KL-SC-PR-004 (all-stainless, detachable) for serious kitchen work.

**Q2: Are your kitchen shears detachable / dishwasher safe?**
A: Yes — our detachable models (KL-SC-PR-011, PR-012) come apart completely for thorough cleaning and are dishwasher-friendly. Our one-piece heavy-duty model (KL-SC-SS-007) is the toughest for cutting through bone but is not detachable. For Damascus and premium finishes, we recommend hand-washing to preserve the coating.

**Q3: What steel do your scissors use?**
A: Volume kitchen shears use 3Cr13/4Cr13 stainless (55–58 HRC); our heavy-duty and garden shears use 5Cr15MoV (57–59 HRC); hairdressing shears use premium hollow-ground stainless at 58–60 HRC. All food-contact models carry SGS LFGB and FDA reports.

**Q4: Do you sell hairdressing / barber scissors?**
A: Yes — KL-SC-HC-002 is our 6.5-inch mirror-polished hairdressing shear with a hollow-ground edge (58–60 HRC), which is the grind type professional barbers require for smooth, snag-free cuts.

**Q5: What's the MOQ for scissors?**
A: Standard volume kitchen shears start at 100 pcs. Light customization (laser logo, custom color) starts at 50–150 pcs with a ~15-day lead time. We welcome mixed containers — pair scissors with our knives and kitchen gadgets to build a full brand.

**Q6: Can these cut through chicken bones?**
A: Our heavy-duty one-piece shears (KL-SC-SS-007) and curved poultry shears (KL-SC-SS-008) are designed for poultry joints and small bones. Do not use standard kitchen shears to cut through thick beef bones or wire — that will damage the blades.

**Q7: Are your scissors food-contact safe?**
A: Yes. All kitchen shears that contact food are made from food-grade stainless steel. Our main factory holds SGS LFGB food-contact compliance and US FDA lead-migration certification, and SGS 21-item heavy-metal migration tests all came back ND.

**Q8: How do I maintain my scissors?**
A: Wipe blades dry after use, especially when cutting acidic or wet materials. Don't cut materials beyond the rated capacity (no wire, no thick bone with light shears). If play develops at the pivot, tighten the center screw. For hairdressing shears, use a professional sharpening service. A light oil on the pivot periodically extends life.
