# 实战剧本03：样品处理与寄样剧本

> **客户画像**：Emily Carter，Founder of "North & Nest"（美国波特兰DTC厨具品牌，主打北美中产家庭，独立站+Amazon。首单目标1000-2000pcs，需要OEM定制胡桃木手柄+激光logo+专属礼盒）。
> **目标产品**：KL-KN-DS-016（67层大马士革钢西式主厨刀黑檀柄，$21基准，MOQ 100pcs）+ 定制胡桃木手柄 + "North & Nest"激光logo。
> **链路**：客户索要免费样品 → 我方样品政策回复 → 客户还价/同意付费 → 样品PI → 生产打样 → 发货 → tracking → 到达 → 反馈 → 大货订单。

## Round 4 — 我方折中方案（不全免，但给诚意）

> Hi Emily,
>
> I respect that you're bootstrapping — and a 1,000-pc first order is a real commitment.
>
> Here's what I can do:
>
> - **1 plain stock sample of KL-KN-DS-016**: I'll cover the sample cost ($25) — no charge.
> - **1 custom-logo sample**: the $50 laser setup fee still applies (it's a real machine setup cost), but it's fully deductible from your first order.
> - **Shipping**: Use your FedEx account (collect) — send me your FedEx account number and I'll arrange pickup from our factory.
>
> So your out-of-pocket cost is just the $50 laser setup fee (which comes back to you on the first order), and whatever FedEx charges you on your own account.
>
> This is the best I can do — the sample knives themselves cost real steel, and I have to be fair to the factory. But I think this works for both of us.
>
> Sound good?
>
> — Leo
