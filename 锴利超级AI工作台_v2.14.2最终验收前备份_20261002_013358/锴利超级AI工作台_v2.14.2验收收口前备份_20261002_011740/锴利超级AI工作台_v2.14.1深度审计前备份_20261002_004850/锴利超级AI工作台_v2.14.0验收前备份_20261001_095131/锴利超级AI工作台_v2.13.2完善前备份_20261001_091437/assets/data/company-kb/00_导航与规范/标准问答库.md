## 二、产品类（Product）

### Q16. What steel grades do you offer? / 你们用什么钢材？
**A:** From entry 3Cr13 (55–57 HRC) to premium VG10 Damascus (65–67 HRC). Our workhorse is 5Cr15MoV (57–59 HRC). We also offer 9Cr18MoV, 440C, D2, German 1.4116, and more.

### Q17. What is 5Cr15MoV? / 5Cr15MoV是什么钢？

### Q18. What is VG10 Damascus? / VG10大马士革是什么？

### Q20. What is Full Tang? / 什么是全龙骨？
