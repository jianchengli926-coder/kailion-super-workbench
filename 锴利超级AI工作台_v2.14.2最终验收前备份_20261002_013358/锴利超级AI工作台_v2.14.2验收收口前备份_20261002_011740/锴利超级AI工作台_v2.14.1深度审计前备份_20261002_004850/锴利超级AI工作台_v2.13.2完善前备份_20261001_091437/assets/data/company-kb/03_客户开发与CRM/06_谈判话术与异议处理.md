# 谈判话术与异议处理

> 外贸谈判常见5大异议：价格、MOQ、交期、质量疑虑、竞品对比。
> 原则：不硬碰硬，先认同再给方案，用数据和证据说话。

---

## 一、价格异议

### 场景1：客户说"Your price is too high"

**话术1（价值锚定）**：
> "I understand price is important. Let's compare the total cost: our knives use 5Cr15MoV stainless steel with HRC 56-58, tested by SGS to LFGB standards. Cheaper options may use 3Cr13 steel which dulls 2-3x faster. Over 10,000 knives, the cost per year of sharpness is actually lower with us."

**话术2（阶梯降价）**：
> "The price for 500pcs is $X.00. If you can increase the order to 1,000pcs, we can reduce the unit price to $Y.00. At 5,000pcs, it goes down to $Z.00. Would a larger quantity work for your plan?"

**话术3（减配置降价）**：
> "If budget is tight, we can offer an alternative: standard polypropylene handle instead of the walnut wood handle, which saves 15%. The blade steel and quality remain the same. Would you like to see that option?"

**话术4（TCO总成本）**：
> "Let's look at total landed cost. We include custom laser logo, color box packaging, and pre-shipment QC in our price. Some quotes may look cheaper but add these later. Our price is all-in."

---

## 二、MOQ异议

### 场景：客户说"Your MOQ is too high, we only need 100pcs"

**话术1（灵活MOQ）**：
> "For this model, our standard MOQ is 500pcs. However, as a new customer, we can accept a trial order of 100pcs for existing models (no custom logo). This lets you test the market first. Would 100pcs work?"

**话术2（混批）**：
> "We can offer mixed models: 100pcs total across 3-4 different knife styles. This way you can test multiple products in one order."

**话术3（现货）**：
> "Actually, we have some stock of this model. If you're OK with standard packaging and no custom logo, we can ship 100pcs immediately."

---

## 三、交期异议

### 场景：客户说"Can you deliver faster? We need it in 2 weeks"

**话术1（诚实说明）**：
> "For custom OEM orders, production takes 30-35 days after deposit. For in-stock models, we can ship in 3-5 days. If timing is critical, I recommend starting with our existing stock, then placing the custom order for next batch."

**话术2（分批发货）**：
> "We can split shipment: ship 200pcs from stock in 1 week, and the remaining 800pcs after production in 35 days. This way you can start selling immediately. Does that work?"

**话术3（加急）**：
> "We can expedite production to 25 days with a 5% rush fee. Would you like me to arrange that?"

---

## 四、质量疑虑

### 场景：客户说"How do I know your quality is good?" / "We had bad experience with Chinese suppliers"

**话术1（检测报告）**：
> "I completely understand. That's why we provide third-party test reports: SGS LFGB food contact certification, TÜV SÜD blade testing, and FDA lead migration test. I'll attach them to this email. You can also request a pre-shipment inspection by BV/SGS before we ship."

**话术2（样品先行）**：
> "The best way to judge quality is to feel it. I can send you a sample DHL tomorrow. You can test the sharpness, balance, and finish yourself. Sample cost is deductible from your first order."

**话术3（工厂背书）**：
> "We work with 4 family factories in Yangjiang that have been manufacturing cutlery for decades. Our steel is from Outokumpu (Germany), with EN 10204-3.1 material certificates. We've supplied brands in Europe and the US."

---

## 五、竞品对比异议

### 场景：客户说"Your competitor offered me a lower price"

**话术1（不贬低对手）**：
> "I respect [Competitor Name]. Different suppliers have different strengths. May I ask: what specifications are they offering? Steel grade, hardness, handle material, certifications? Sometimes prices differ because the base materials are different."

**话术2（差异化价值）**：
> "If the specs are identical, I'll do my best to match. But what we offer beyond price: (1) 1-on-1 dedicated support, (2) pre-production QC video approval, (3) 100% pre-shipment inspection, (4) replacement warranty for defective units. Most issues happen after delivery — we stand behind our products."

**话术3（长期合作）**：
> "For a first order, a few cents difference is small. What matters more is consistent quality and reliable delivery over 2 years. We aim to be your long-term partner, not a one-time supplier."

## 六、已读不回追问话术

### ABC选项法
> "Seems we get stuck somewhere. In order to move forward, could you give me feedback on:
> A. Price or payment term issue?
> B. Shipping/freight issue?
> C. Any other reasons?
> Any feedback helps me improve our offer."

## 九、谈判底线参考

| 条款 | 可接受范围 | 底线 |
|---|---|---|
| 价格 | 标准价±10% | 不低于成本价+5% |
| MOQ | 标准MOQ的20% | 不低于50pcs |
| 定金 | 30% | 不低于20% |
| 付款方式 | T/T, L/C | 不接受100% OA（除非中信保） |
| 交期 | 标准35天±10天 | 不接受短于25天的定制单 |
| 样品费 | 可免/可抵 | 定制样品必须收打样费 |

> **注意**：以上为内部参考，实际根据客户等级和订单量灵活调整。A类大客户可适当放宽条件。
