# Platform Selection & Onboarding Decision / 平台选择与入驻决策

## 2. KaiLionCrafts Onboarding Priority / 入驻优先级建议

### Priority Tier 1 (Now — Months 0-3)

1. **Shopify DTC (KaiLionCrafts.com)** — already in progress per 05_独立站与SEO. Brand control, highest margin, no marketplace knife restrictions beyond payment processors.
2. **Amazon US Professional Seller** — primary demand channel for kitchen knives. FBA for hero SKUs.

### Priority Tier 2 (Months 3-6)

3. **eBay US Basic Store** — test long-tail SKUs (shears, peeler, vintage-style fixed blades), collectible angle.
4. Begin **Brand Registry** process (USPTO trademark pending).

### Priority Tier 3 (Months 6-12)

5. **Walmart Marketplace** — once US EIN entity set up, existing Amazon proof of sales, and WFS relationship established.
6. **Etsy** — only for hand-forged / artisanal / Damascus-line SKUs (aligns with 阳江 craftsmanship story).

### Do NOT Prioritize

- eBay UK / eBay DE / eBay AU for knives (policy bans kitchen knives on UK; assisted in AU).
- Amazon UK/EU until VAT + OAV + local entity ready.

## 4. Team Configuration / 多平台运营团队配置

### Stage 1: 0-6 months (OPC / solo founder + 1 VA)

- Founder: strategy, listings, ads, supplier negotiation.
- Part-time VA (Philippines/China): customer service, order processing, listing upload.
- US 3PL handles fulfillment.

### Stage 2: 6-18 months (3-5 people)

| Role | Headcount | Responsibility |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Operations Lead | 1 | Amazon + Walmart strategy, ads, P&L |
| Content / Listing Specialist | 1 | Copywriting, A+ content, images, SEO |
| Customer Service / VA | 1 | Messages, returns, review outreach |
| Supply Chain / Warehousing | 1 (part-time) | 阳江→US freight, 3PL coordination |
| Founder | 1 | Brand, partnerships, finance |

### Stage 3: 18+ months

- Add dedicated **PPC/Ads specialist** once ad spend >$5k/mo.
- Add **Data analyst** for inventory forecasting when SKU count >200.

---

## 5. Decision Triggers / 何时扩平台

Add a new platform only when **all** of these are true:

- [ ] Current top channel has >50 SKUs live.
- [ ] BSR / sales rank stable for 30+ days.
- [ ] Cash flow positive (not burning runway).
- [ ] Compliance checklist for that platform signed off.
- [ ] Existing ops can handle 20% more order volume without dropping SLA.
