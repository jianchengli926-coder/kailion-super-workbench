# Outdoor Knives FAQ — KaiLionCrafts

> ⚠️ **REGULATORY WARNING: Knife import laws differ by country. Confirm destination-country regulations before shipment.** Automatic/OTF/switchblade knives and balisong/butterfly knives are prohibited in nearly all major markets. Blade length limits, locking mechanisms, and double-edged designs are heavily regulated in the EU, UK, Australia, Canada, Japan, and others. This FAQ does not constitute legal advice. Always have your destination customs broker confirm in writing before shipping.

**Q: What is the difference between a fixed blade and a folding knife?**
**A:** Fixed-blade knives (fixed-blade-hunting-knives, 10 SKUs) have a non-folding blade — typically Full Tang, strongest for heavy outdoor/bushcraft/hunting use, and come with a leather sheath. Folding pocket knives (folding-pocket-edc-knives, 12 SKUs) fold into the handle for everyday carry (EDC), typically with a liner lock or frame lock and pocket clip.

**Q: ⚠️ What is the maximum blade length allowed in Europe?**
**A:** Limits vary by country: Germany — fixed blade >12 cm prohibited in public carry (import for private/collection use may differ); UK — folding pocket knife with cutting edge ≤3" (7.62 cm) and NO lock qualifies as a pocket knife (locked folders are treated as offensive weapons); Italy — folding knife with lock ≥5 cm requires justified reason; Spain — non-automatic folding knife >11 cm prohibited for sale; Japan — locked folding knife ≥5.5 cm requires public safety commission permit. **We recommend European customers select fixed blades ≤8 cm or non-locked folders ≤5 cm.**
- 技术依据：`04_国家地区开发指南/11_户外刀各国进口法规专项.md` 欧洲各国各节

**Q: ⚠️ What is the HS code for outdoor knives?**

**Q: How do I care for high-carbon outdoor knives?**
**A:** High-carbon steel (HC series) is not stainless — it will rust if left wet. Wipe the blade dry after every use, apply a light coat of oil (bore oil or food-safe mineral oil) for storage, and store in a dry place. A patina will develop over time, which is normal and actually helps protect the blade. Damascus and D2 also require regular drying and oiling.

**Q: What is Full Tang and why is it important for outdoor knives?**
**A:** Full Tang means the steel blade runs the full length of the handle, with handle scales on both sides. It is the defining structural feature of a heavy-duty fixed-blade hunting knife — maximum strength, balance, and reliability in the field. All our HC-series fixed-blade hunting knives are Full Tang.

**Q: ⚠️ Can you ship to Australia? What are the rules?**
**A:** Australia prohibits import of automatic/flick/switchblade/assisted-opening knives, balisong/butterfly knives, daggers, throwing knives, push knives, and ceramic/undetectable knives. However, **manual single-edged folding pocket knives are NOT on the prohibited imports list** (Schedule 13) and can be imported at the federal level with no blade-length limit. Fixed-blade hunting knives can also be imported. We recommend Australian customers select manual single-edge folders or fixed-blade bushcraft/hunting knives only.
- 技术依据：`04_国家地区开发指南/11_户外刀各国进口法规专项.md` 澳大利亚节
- 关联SKU：KL-OD-SS-006 (manual folder)

**Q: ⚠️ Can you ship to Japan?**
**A:** Japan's sword-and-firearms control law is strict. Fixed blades ≥15 cm require a public safety committee permit. Locked folding knives ≥5.5 cm are classified as "tōjūrui" (swords/blades) requiring a permit. Automatic knives (hbidashi-knives) are prohibited regardless of length. We recommend Japanese customers select non-locked folding knives under 5.5 cm or confirm their permit status before ordering.
- 技术依据：`04_国家地区开发指南/11_户外刀各国进口法规专项.md` 日本节

**Q: ⚠️ Do you ship to the Middle East (UAE / Saudi)?**
**A:** Caution required. In the UAE, hunting/tactical knives are on multiple carriers' restricted list for express shipping; household kitchen knives ship normally. Saudi Arabia requires SABER certification for all imports; self-locking folders and long outdoor knives may be seized without proper documentation. We recommend Middle East customers: (1) use B2B formal customs clearance with a licensed local importer, (2) obtain SABER for Saudi shipments, (3) avoid hunting/tactical B2C direct mail.
