# eBay Operations Guide / eBay 运营指南

> 说明（中文）：eBay 是 KaiLionCrafts 测试长尾 SKU、处理清库存、触达北美收藏家/户外爱好者的补充渠道。美国站规则相对宽松，英国站几乎全面禁刀，需分站点运营。

---

## 1. Account Types & Store Levels / 账号类型与店铺等级

### 1.1 Registration

- Individual seller → no monthly fee; 250 zero-fee listings/month (auction-style).
- Business account: required if selling >$50k/year or using business name; allows tax-exempt sales and company branding.

### 1.2 eBay Store Subscriptions (US)

Source: https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/payments-and-fees/subscriptions-and-fees (retrieved 2026-09-27)

| Store Tier | Monthly (annual) | Free Fixed-Price Listings | Best For |
|------------|------------------|---------------------------|----------|
| No Store | $0 | 250 auction/mo | Casual sellers |
| Starter | $4.95/mo | 250/mo | Small catalog |
| Basic | $21.95/mo | 1,000/mo | Growing business |
| Premium | $59.95/mo (annual) | 10,000/mo | Established catalog |
| Anchor | $299.95/mo | 100,000/mo | Enterprise |
| Enterprise | $2,999.95/mo | 250,000/mo | Large retailers |

- **Final Value Fee (FVF)**: ~13.25% of total sale + $0.30 per order (Feb 2025 adjustment up to +0.35% in most categories).
- **KaiLionCrafts recommendation**: Start **Basic Store** ($21.95/mo) once 50+ SKUs live.

### 1.3 Top Rated Seller (TRS) Requirements

Source: https://export.ebay.com/en/growth/seller-performance/top-rated-seller/ (retrieved 2026-09-27)

- Account active ≥90 days.
- ≥100 transactions in trailing 12 months (US/Canada/UK buyers).
- **Transaction defect rate ≤0.5%**.
- **Cases closed without seller resolution ≤2 (or ≤0.3%)**.
- On-time tracking ≥95%.
- **Benefit**: +10% top-rated seller discount on FVF, better search visibility, Trusted review badge.

## 2. Auction vs Fixed Price / 拍卖 vs 一口价

### 2.1 When to Use Which

| Format | Best For | Duration |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **Auction-style** | Rare/collectible/vintage knives, test market price | 3/5/7/10 days; end Sunday evening |
| **Fixed Price (Buy It Now)** | Standard SKUs, repurchasable products | GTC (Good Till Cancelled) |
| **Auction + Best Offer** | Negotiation-friendly collectibles | - |

### 2.2 Pricing Strategy

- Start auction at **$0.99 opening bid** for visibility (only if floor price acceptable).
- For KaiLionCrafts new SKUs: Fixed Price + Best Offer; run 1-week auctions on 3-5 hero SKUs to gather price data.
- **Best time to list**: Sunday evening 6-10 PM ET (US weekend bidding peak).

### 2.3 Listing Quality

- Title ≤80 chars, front-load keywords: `Brand Knife Type Steel Material Use`.
- Item specifics: fill 100% (blade material, blade length, handle material, opening mechanism, closed length).
- Photos: 12 free; use 8-12; include ruler for scale on blade length.

## 3. Logistics / 物流方案

### 3.1 eBay International Shipping (EIS) — successor to GSP

Source: https://export.ebay.com/en/fees-and-payments/seller-fees/selling-fees-for-managed-payments-sellers/ (retrieved 2026-09-27)

- Seller ships order to a US domestic hub; eBay handles international customs/duties/final delivery.
- No international fee charged when using EIS on eligible listings.
- Without EIS: international fee applies when buyer ship-to or registered address is outside US.
- **刀具注意**: EIS cannot ship knives to UK/DE/AU sites due to local bans — set shipping exclusions.

### 3.2 Domestic Shipping

- USPS Priority Mail: best for small knife parcels.
- Require **signature confirmation** on blade products (age verification + loss prevention).
- Handling time: set 1 business day to keep defect rate low.

## 4. Buyer & Seller Protection / 买卖家保护

- **eBay Money Back Guarantee**: buyer can claim item not as described / not received.
- Seller protection applies if: tracking shows delivered, dispatched within handling time, item matches description.
- **Top Rated Plus**: sellers offering 3-day or faster free shipping qualify for extra protection.
- **中文提示**：刀具类退货要检查刃口是否损坏、是否被使用；一旦开刃过就不能二次销售，记录开箱视频。

## 5. ⚠️ eBay Weapons & Knives Policy / 刀具类目政策（重点）

### 5.1 eBay US — Prohibited Knives

Source: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/knives-policy?id=5047 (retrieved 2026-09-27):

The following **may NOT be listed on eBay.com**:

- Automatic knives / Switchblades
- Butterfly knives (Balisong)
- Dual-action knives
- Gravity knives
- Out-the-front (OTF) knives
- Paratrooper knives
- Push knives
- Sword canes
- Spring-assisted knives **without a clearly visible thumb stud or screw** for manual opening
- Hidden/disguised knives: writing-pen knives, belt-buckle knives, lipstick knives

### 5.2 eBay US — Allowed

- All knives not on the prohibited list, **provided they comply with international trading policy and local laws**.
- Kitchen knives, manual folding knives, fixed-blade hunting/camping knives, multi-tools (Leatherman-style).

### 5.3 eBay UK — Almost Total Knife Ban

Source: https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/knives-policy?id=5047 (retrieved 2026-09-27):

- **Prohibited**: axes as weapons, multi-tools/craft knives/Stanley knives/Leathermans, pocket/folding/Swiss Army/clasp knives, razor/surgical blades, **sharp kitchen knives (block sets, cake/carving/paring knives)**.
- **Implication for KaiLionCrafts**: Do NOT list kitchen knives on eBay.co.uk. The UK market is effectively closed for bladed goods on eBay.

### 5.4 eBay Australia

Source: https://www.ebay.com.au/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/knives-policy?id=5047 (retrieved 2026-09-27):

- Bans: automatic, butterfly, dual-action, gravity, star, trench, OTF, paratrooper, push, switchblade, sword canes, **spring-assisted knives (no thumb-stud exception)**, disguised knives, **zombie knives**.
- Australia is stricter than US — spring-assisted folding knives are banned even with thumb stud.

### 5.5 eBay Germany / EU

Source: https://www.ebay.de/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/grundsatz-zu-messern-und-anderen-hieb-und-stichwaffen?id=5047 (retrieved 2026-09-27):

- Banned: swords, sabers, rapiers, bayonets, Hirschfänger, any weapon under German WaffG, **Springmesser (switchblades) even <8.5 cm blade**, Faustmesser, Balisong/butterfly.
- Spain/France/Italy: blades >11 cm restricted.

### 5.6 Cross-Marketplace Quick Comparison

| Knife Type | eBay US | eBay UK | eBay DE | eBay AU |
|------------|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| Kitchen/chef knife | ✅ | ❌ | ⚠️ | ✅ |
| Manual folding (thumb stud) | ✅ | ❌ | ⚠️ | ✅ |
| Spring-assisted (visible stud) | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Switchblade/automatic/OTF | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Butterfly/balisong | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Machete | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |

### 5.7 Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Listing title: no "tactical", "combat", "self-defense", "zombie".
- [ ] Item Specifics: honestly declare opening mechanism (Manual / Assisted / Automatic).
- [ ] Shipping exclusions: block UK, Germany, Australia for restricted SKUs.
- [ ] Age 18+ handling: require signature on delivery.
- [ ] Do not list on eBay.co.uk for any kitchen knife SKU.
